August 2026 is the eighth month of the current common year. The month, which began on a Saturday, will end on a Monday after 31 days. It is the current month.

2026 Lebanon war
The Israeli military says it has killed several Hezbollah fighters in an Israeli-held area of southern Lebanon, despite a ceasefire framework between Israel and Lebanon. (AFP via South China Morning Post)
Red Sea crisis
The Houthis in Yemen deny reports of imposing tolls on vessels passing through the Bab-el-Mandeb strait. (Al Jazeera)
Russo-Ukrainian war

The Washington Post reports that Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu blocked a request by former United States senator Lindsey Graham to transfer an Iron Dome system from the U.S. to Ukraine. (Haaretz)
Kyiv strikes

Sudanese civil war

Egypt and Sudan reach an agreement on a 16-month plan to rehabilitate the Jebel Aulia Dam south of Khartoum, including the construction of a temporary bridge, after the dam was damaged in November 2023 during shelling by the Rapid Support Forces. (Sudan Tribune)
Terrorism in Colombia
2026 attack on the Norte de Santander Police Command
A truck bomb explodes beside a police station in Cúcuta, Norte de Santander, Colombia, injuring eleven police officers and three civilians. (AFP via France 24)
Terrorism in Russia 
2026 Moscow bombing
A woman attempts to set off a bomb inside a coffeehouse in Moscow, Russia, but is stopped at the entrance and detonates the explosive. Three people are killed, including the perpetrator, and 21 more are injured. (CNN)

Arts and culture
Iraq in the 2026 Iran war
The Arba'in Shia Islam pilgrimage in Iraq is held amidst the Iran war, with Iranian pilgrims attending the event. (Al-Monitor)

Disasters and accidents
2026 United States wildfires
2026 Washington wildfires
The Old Trails wildfire in northwestern Spokane, Washington, United States, burns over 3,000 acres (12 km2), destroying many structures. Residents near Fort Spokane are told to evacuate. (CNN) (News.az)
2024–2026 Cuba blackouts
Cuba reports a partial collapse of the national power grid, leaving western provinces without electricity after transmission lines fail between Matanzas and Pinar del Río. (Xinhua)

2026 Broad Peak avalanche
All ten climbers, including Nirmal Purja, are confirmed deceased after an avalanche at Broad Peak in Pakistan on July 30. (Al Jazeera)

2026 Nepal floods
Flash floods and mudslides along the Trishuli River impact Nepal and Tibet. At least 160 people are killed, hundreds are injured and 463 others, including over 200 tourists, are reported missing in Nepal, while Chinese Communist Party officials report three deaths and 265 missing in Tibet, severely affecting Gyirong County and its port. (AFP via Jamaica Observer) (Nepalkhabar) (Kantipur) (Japan Today)
