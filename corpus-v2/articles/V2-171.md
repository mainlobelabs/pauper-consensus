Iran announces the Strait of Hormuz is closed to traffic over Lebanon ceasefire violations
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 20, 2026 (via Guardian, Euronews, BBC)

The Iranian military command announces that the Strait of Hormuz is closed to traffic because of Israel's violations of the Islamabad Memorandum ceasefire in Lebanon; the IRGC Navy warns ships to stay away from the strait.

Iran's Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters said on Saturday, 20 June 2026, that "the Strait of Hormuz will be closed to vessel traffic," citing US "flagrant bad faith and breach of covenant" under the 14-point memorandum and the "continuous and relentless violations of the ceasefire in southern Lebanon" by Israel. The closure was described as a "first step."

The IRGC Navy said, "vessels should not approach the Strait of Hormuz; otherwise, their security will be at risk." The announcement came after the Israel-Hezbollah ceasefire took effect on Friday and Israel launched fresh strikes on southern Lebanon, killing 16 to 20 people.

The United States disputed the closure. CENTCOM said, "Iran does not control the Strait of Hormuz. Traffic continues to flow," and 55 merchant ships transited the strait on Saturday carrying more than 17 million barrels of oil. The Joint Maritime Information Centre directed traffic on a southern route after a confirmed mine sighting.
