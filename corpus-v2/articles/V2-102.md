Putin offers Trump a ceasefire for Victory Day on May 9, Kremlin says
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 29, 2026 (via NPR, Kyiv Independent)

During a phone call with US president Donald Trump, Russian president Vladimir Putin offered a ceasefire for Victory Day on May 9. On Wednesday, April 29, 2026, the Kremlin said the call lasted more than 90 minutes, was initiated by Russia, and was the first publicly confirmed conversation between the two leaders since March 9.

Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Putin informed Trump of Russia's "readiness to declare a ceasefire for the period of the Victory Day celebrations," which include a military parade on Red Square on May 9. According to the Kremlin, Trump "actively supported" the idea, saying he "shared victory over Nazism." Trump, who pressed for the pause, said, "I suggested a little bit of a ceasefire... I think he might do that."

The two also discussed Iran, with Putin offering help with enriched uranium, and Trump said, "I want to end your war," indicating a preference for Russia to focus on Ukraine. Trump claimed that Ukraine was "militarily defeated" and that a deal was "close." Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelensky was not consulted, and he asked, "a few hours of security for a parade in Moscow, or something more?" and sought clarification, wanting a long-term, guaranteed ceasefire.

The backdrop is Putin's 2025 Victory Day truce, which ran May 8-11 and during which Ukraine counted more than 734 violations between 00:00 and noon on May 8. The 2026 parade is expected to be scaled down because of the drone threat.
