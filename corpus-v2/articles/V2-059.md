Yemen's Houthis fire ballistic missile at Israel, entering Iran war
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 28, 2026 (via Times of Israel, France 24)

Yemen's Houthis have launched a ballistic missile at Israel, marking the group's entry into the war between Iran and the United States and Israel.

The missile, fired on Saturday, March 28, 2026, was aimed at the southern Israeli city of Beersheba. Israel said it intercepted the missile, with no casualties or damage reported. Houthi spokesman Yahya Sarea announced the attack on the group's Al Masirah TV channel.

Hours later, the Houthis mounted a second attack, firing a cruise missile and drones at the southern port of Eilat, all of which Israel said were shot down. Both attacks came without reported injuries or damage on the ground.

The Houthis, backed by Iran, have attacked shipping in the Red Sea since late 2023 in support of Palestinians in Gaza, and their entry into the current war brings a new front to the conflict. The strikes test Israel's air defenses along its southern border, and they are the first Houthi attacks on Israeli territory in support of Iran. The Houthis have controlled much of Yemen's territory, including its port of Hodeidah, since their rise to power, and their missile stockpile has grown with Iranian support.
