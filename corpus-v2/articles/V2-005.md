Tarique Rahman sworn in as Bangladesh's 11th prime minister
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 17, 2026 (via Reuters, The Guardian)

Bangladesh Nationalist Party leader Tarique Rahman was sworn in as the 11th prime minister of Bangladesh on Tuesday, February 17, 2026, following his party's victory in the recent election.

President Mohammed Shahabuddin administered the oath at parliament, in a ceremony held outdoors in a departure from tradition. Rahman unveiled a government of 25 cabinet ministers and 24 junior ministers.

The BNP won a two-thirds majority in the election, returning the party to power after roughly two decades in opposition. The new government succeeds an 18-month caretaker administration led by Muhammad Yunus, the Nobel laureate economist who served as the country's ninth prime minister, with the preceding caretaker government serving as the tenth.

The two-thirds parliamentary majority gives the government the power to amend the constitution, a mandate that underscores the scale of the party's electoral victory. Rahman, the son of former prime minister Ziaur Rahman, is the son of the BNP founder and had previously led the party through its opposition years.

Rahman's return to the premiership caps a period of political transition in Bangladesh, which had been governed by caretaker administrations since the fall of the previous elected government. His government will take office with a commanding parliamentary mandate.
