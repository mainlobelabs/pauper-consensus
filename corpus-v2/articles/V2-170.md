Poland strips Zelenskyy of the Order of the White Eagle over a UPA unit naming
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 19, 2026 (via president.pl, AP, BBC)

Polish president Karol Nawrocki strips Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy of Poland's highest honour, the Order of the White Eagle, for naming an army unit after the Ukrainian Insurgent Army, which massacred Poles during World War II.

In a 13-minute video address on Friday, 19 June 2026, Nawrocki said that, "after consulting with the Chapter, I have decided to revoke the Order of the White Eagle from the President of Ukraine," following Zelenskyy's consent to name one of the units of the Armed Forces of Ukraine "Heroes of the UPA."

The unit, part of Ukraine's special operations forces, was named in a decree on 26 May 2026. The UPA is accused in Poland of WWII massacres, especially in Volhynia and Eastern Galicia, where an estimated 100,000 Polish civilians were killed. Nawrocki called the decision "outrageous" and said, "Historical truth is not, and can never be, a bargaining chip."

He said the move is not directed at the Ukrainian people and does not change Poland's support for Ukraine. Prime minister Donald Tusk appealed "not to waste" ally solidarity, saying Zelenskyy did not intend to offend but "lacks sensitivity."

Zelenskyy gave no direct comment. His chief of staff Andriy Sybiha called the revocation "a strategic mistake by the President of Poland." The decision comes days before a Ukraine recovery conference in Gdansk.
