Trump dismisses Kristi Noem as homeland security secretary
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 5, 2026 (via AP, CNBC)

United States president Donald Trump has dismissed Kristi Noem from the post of secretary of Homeland Security and nominated Oklahoma senator Markwayne Mullin to replace her.

The dismissal was announced on Thursday, March 5, 2026. Mullin, a Republican, is a senator from Oklahoma and must be confirmed by the Senate before he can take up the post. His start date, pending confirmation, is set for March 31, which would leave the department's leadership in a transition period for several weeks.

Noem has been named special envoy for "Shield of the Americas," a new role announced alongside the dismissal that focuses on security cooperation with the region's neighbors. The change followed Noem's recent testimony before Congress about a $220 million advertising campaign by the Department of Homeland Security, which drew criticism over its cost and content.

Noem, who served as the governor of South Dakota before joining the cabinet, is one of a small number of senior cabinet members to leave the administration in the current period. The White House did not give a detailed reason for the change. Mullin has represented Oklahoma in the Senate, where he served on committees touching on national security and domestic affairs.
