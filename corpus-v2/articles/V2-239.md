Pentagon releases more classified UFO files, including 2021 Gulf of Oman cold-orbs footage
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 7, 2026 (via Scientific American, BBC News, UFO Releases)

The US Department of Defense has released more classified videos and files of unidentified flying objects, including a 2021 incident in the Gulf of Oman in which US Air Force pilots encountered a swarm of cold orbs.

The release, the fifth in a series of declassification drops the Pentagon has made since 2024, included an Intelligence Information Report issued in April 2022 by Air Force Special Operations Command.

The report documents an 8 September 2021 observation by an AC-130J gunship crew during a night live-fire exercise over the Gulf of Oman. The crew recorded some 25 instances of a UAP described as a cold orb roughly 4 feet, or 1.2 metres, in diameter, moving in irregular patterns from 0 to 20 feet up.

Two of the orbs reportedly fled at the moment the aircraft's cannons fired. The footage was captured on a mobile device showing the gunship's infrared display.

The release also included a triangle sighting in Afghanistan and new files from Colorado. The Pentagon's UAP program, established to catalogue sightings, has released batches of declassified material in recent years in response to congressional and public pressure for transparency over encounters with unexplained aerial phenomena.
