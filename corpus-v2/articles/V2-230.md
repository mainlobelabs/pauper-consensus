New clashes reported across Tigray Region as hundreds flee to Sudan
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 2, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, BBC, Mada Masr, Wikipedia)

New clashes have been reported across the Tigray Region in northern Ethiopia between the Ethiopian federal army and the Tigray People's Liberation Front, four years after the previous war in the region ended under the Pretoria Agreement of November 2022. The fighting has prompted hundreds of civilians to flee across the border into Sudan, amidst fears of a new civil war.

The clashes, reported over the weekend of 31 July, were concentrated in western Tigray around Shererina, near the town of Humera. Tigray Defense Forces launched a push against positions held by the Ethiopian National Defense Force, and the federal army said the next day that it had repelled the advance and secured the area, claiming an Ethiopian victory.

On the day of the attack, Ethiopian artillery hit refugee camps on the Sudanese side of the border, residents and aid workers reported. Al Jazeera reported that Tigray refugees were fleeing into Sudan after the deadly border violence. The TPLF later said it de-escalated after the refugee exodus, and clashes were also reported along the Eritrean border.

The latest fighting follows the January 2026 Tigray clashes and has rekindled fears of a wider regional war. The 2022 Pretoria Agreement ended a two-year war between the federal government and the Tigray leadership, but the two sides continue to dispute its implementation, and the United Nations has called for restraint as the number of displaced people grows.
