Linda Nosková wins her first major title at Wimbledon
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 11, 2026 (via WTA, AP, BBC, Wimbledon.com)

In tennis, Linda Nosková has won her first major title after defeating her compatriot Karolína Muchová in the final 6-2, 5-7, 6-3 at the 2026 Wimbledon Championships.

The 21-year-old, seeded ninth, overcame a 2-5 deficit in the second set after Muchová, the 10th seed, saved three championship points on her serve and broke to win five straight games. Nosková regrouped and took an early break in the decider, converting the sixth championship point on her serve, and dropped to the grass. Muchová had been broken only twice in her prior three matches before the final.

The all-Czech final made Nosková the youngest women's Wimbledon champion since 21-year-old Petra Kvitová in 2011, and the third Czech woman to win the title in four years, after Markéta Vondroušová in 2023 and Barbora Krejčíková in 2024. She became the third player to win the SW19 Open after saving match point earlier the same fortnight.

Nosková dedicated the title to her mother Ivana, who died of cancer on the eve of Wimbledon 2024. It was her eighth career final. Muchová, 29, reached her second Grand Slam final after losing to Iga Świątek at Roland Garros in 2023.
