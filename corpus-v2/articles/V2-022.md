Iran confirms deaths of four senior military officials in Tehran strike
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 1, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, Iran International)

Iran has confirmed the deaths of four senior officials who were killed in an airstrike on a Defense Council meeting in Tehran.

The confirmations, announced on Sunday, March 1, 2026, name Abdolrahim Mousavi, the armed forces chief of staff, Aziz Nasirzadeh, the defense minister, Mohammad Pakpour, the commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, and Ali Shamkhani, the secretary of the Defense Council. The strike hit the meeting on the first day of the war between Iran and the United States and Israel.

The deaths represent the loss of a senior group of commanders in a single blow, and they deepen the leadership crisis in Iran's military and security establishment as the war continues. The Defense Council coordinates the country's military planning, and its members are among the most senior figures in the state's security apparatus.

The confirmations came as the war entered its fourth day, with the interim leadership council, formed after the death of supreme leader Ali Khamenei on February 28, still governing the country. The four officials had each held one of the most powerful security posts in the state, and their deaths in a single meeting are the most significant loss of command personnel reported so far in the conflict.
