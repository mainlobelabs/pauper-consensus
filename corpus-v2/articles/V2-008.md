Trump orders release of classified files on extraterrestrial life, UAPs
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 19, 2026 (via Reuters, The Wall Street Journal)

United States president Donald Trump has ordered defense secretary Pete Hegseth and other relevant department heads to begin the process of releasing classified government files related to extraterrestrial life and unidentified anomalous phenomena.

In a post on Truth Social on Thursday, February 19, 2026, Trump directed the release of files on "alien and extraterrestrial life, UAP and UFOs," with Hegseth and agency heads tasked with beginning the declassification process.

The order followed comments by former president Barack Obama on a podcast, in which the former president discussed extraterrestrial life and the government's handling of UAP reports, fueling renewed public interest in the subject.

The UAP topic has received growing attention from the U.S. military and Congress in recent years, with lawmakers from both parties pressing for greater transparency about sightings reported by pilots and sensor systems. The defense department has previously released a small number of UAP videos and reports to the public.

The files are expected to originate from multiple agencies, with the defense department leading the release effort. The move is the latest in a series of presidential actions on the topic of unidentified anomalous phenomena.
