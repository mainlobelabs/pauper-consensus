Malawi beats Ghana 2-1 at Women's Africa Cup of Nations to reach the World Cup for the first time
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 9, 2026 (via ESPN, CAF, FIFA, Reuters)

In association football, Malawi's women's national team has defeated Ghana 2-1 in the quarter-finals of the Women's Africa Cup of Nations, held in Morocco, to qualify for the upcoming FIFA Women's World Cup in Brazil, where they will make their tournament debut.

The Scorchers, ranked 153rd, fell behind before Rose Kadzere scored a late winner at the Al Medina Stadium in Rabat to end the Black Queens' hopes. The victory was the culmination of a fairytale run for one of Africa's smallest football programmes.

Qualification for the 2027 World Cup in Brazil came with a place in the Africa Cup of Nations semi-finals, which had been granted World Cup berths. Malawi became the first team from their confederation region to reach the semi-finals and the first to qualify for the women's World Cup.

Ghana, who missed out on automatic qualification, will contest a playoff route against Cote d'Ivoire for a 2027 World Cup place. The tournament, hosted by Morocco, has awarded World Cup qualification to its semi-finalists since the 2022 edition.

Malawi's achievement was described by FIFA as a landmark for women's football on the continent, and the squad was welcomed home as heroes by a nation that has long been overlooked in the sport.
