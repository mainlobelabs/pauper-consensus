United States beats Canada 2-1 in overtime for men's hockey gold in Milano Cortina
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 22, 2026 (via The Guardian, BBC)

The United States defeated Canada 2-1 in overtime on Sunday, February 22, 2026, to win the men's ice hockey gold medal at the Milano Cortina Winter Olympics, its first in 46 years.

Jack Hughes scored the winning goal 101 seconds into the extra period to seal the victory. The win is the third gold medal for the United States men's team, joining the titles of 1960 and 1980, and the first won on foreign ice.

The 1980 "Miracle on Ice" victory, in which the U.S. defeated the Soviet Union, came exactly 46 years earlier and remains one of the most famous upsets in Olympic history.

The Olympic tournament, staged in Milano Cortina, brought together twelve national teams, and the gold medal game was the final event of the competition. The matchup drew large audiences in North America, where the men's final is one of the most-watched events of the Games. The United States team celebrated on the ice after the final buzzer. United States goaltender Connor Hellebuyck delivered a stellar performance in net throughout the final.

The team paid tribute to Johnny Gaudreau during the celebrations after the final whistle.
