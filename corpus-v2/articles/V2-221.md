Fields Medals 2026 awarded to Yu Deng, John Pardon, Jacob Tsimerman, Hong Wang
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 23, 2026 (via IMU, Simons Foundation, Scientific American, Nature)

The International Mathematical Union has awarded the 2026 Fields Medals to Yu Deng, John Pardon, Jacob Tsimerman, and Hong Wang at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Philadelphia.

At the opening ceremony of ICM 2026 at the Pennsylvania Convention Center, the IMU announced the medals. Yu Deng of the University of Chicago was honoured for the rigorous derivation of the Boltzmann equation from rarefied gas dynamics. John Pardon of Stony Brook and the Simons Center was recognised for symplectic geometry, including virtual fundamental cycles, and knot theory.

Jacob Tsimerman of the University of Toronto was honoured for work on o-minimality in arithmetic and complex algebraic geometry, including the Griffiths conjecture. Hong Wang of NYU and IHES was recognised for algebraic geometry.

Wang becomes the third woman to receive the prize, after Maryam Mirzakhani in 2014 and Maryna Viazovska in 2022. Deng and Wang were 2026 Clay Research Awardees, and Pardon served as a Clay Research Fellow from 2015 to 2020. Deng and Wang are the second and third Chinese nationals to receive the medal, after Shing-Tung Yau in 1982. The congress runs from 23 to 30 July in Philadelphia.
