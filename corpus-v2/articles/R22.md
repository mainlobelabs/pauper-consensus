World Cup debutants Curaçao earn their first point with a 0-0 draw against Ecuador
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 20, 2026 (via FIFA, Guardian, ESPN)

In association football, FIFA World Cup debutants Curaçao, the smallest nation by population to play at the World Cup, earn their first-ever point at the tournament after drawing 0-0 against Ecuador in the group stage.

The Group E match was played at Kansas City Stadium on Saturday, 20 June 2026, in front of 68,598 fans. Curaçao, with a population of about 158,000 and ranked 81st, is the smallest nation by population to have reached a World Cup finals.

Goalkeeper Eloy Room, 37, made 15 saves, a new record for the most stops in 90 minutes of a World Cup match since 1966. He said, "It means everything... the first point at the World Cup."

Curaçao, coached by Dick Advocaat, was recovering from a 7-1 opening loss to Germany and still had to beat Ivory Coast in Philadelphia on 25 June to advance. Germany topped Group E after a comeback win against Ivory Coast.

Ecuador, with one point from two matches, also had to beat Germany on 25 June to advance. They had lost 1-0 to Ivory Coast while registering 27 shots, and fans jeered them off the field.
