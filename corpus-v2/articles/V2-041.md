One Battle After Another takes Best Picture at the Oscars
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 15, 2026 (via Hollywood Reporter, CNBC)

One Battle After Another, the latest film from director Paul Thomas Anderson, has won Best Picture at the 98th Academy Awards on Sunday, March 15, 2026.

The film won a total of six awards. Anderson took home Best Director, his first win in the category, and the film won Best Adapted Screenplay. Sean Penn won Best Supporting Actor, and Cassandra Kulukundis received the film's casting honor, the inaugural Achievement in Casting award introduced this year. The film also won Best Film Editing.

Anderson, known for films including There Will Be Blood and Phantom Thread, is among the most acclaimed directors working in cinema, and the Best Director win caps a long run of critical recognition for his body of work.

The new casting award recognizes the work of professional casting directors, who until this year had no dedicated category at the Oscars. Casting directors have long been central to Hollywood productions, shaping the chemistry of an ensemble cast, but they have historically been recognized only through honorary or side awards. The film's win was the biggest of a night in which it competed across several of the ceremony's major categories.
