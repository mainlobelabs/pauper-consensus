South Korea Supreme Court upholds Kweon Seong-dong's prison sentence over Unification Church funds
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 16, 2026 (via Yonhap, Korea Herald, JoongAng Daily)

South Korea's Supreme Court has upheld People Power Party lawmaker Kweon Seong-dong's two-year prison sentence for accepting 100 million won (US$67,400) in illegal political funds from the Unification Church, removing him from the National Assembly.

The court's second division upheld the first and appellate court rulings of a two-year term plus a 100 million won forfeit for violating the Political Funds Act. The finalised forfeit strips him of his seat under the National Assembly Act.

Kweon, a five-term lawmaker and former PPP floor leader, accepted the 100 million won on 5 January 2022 from Yun Young-ho, then head of the Unification Church world headquarters, with a request that the Yoon administration support the denomination ahead of the 20th presidential election. A special counsel team led by Min Joong-ki sought four years.

Key evidence included Yun's diary entry and his message: "What I gave you today is small, but I hope you will use it helpfully for the presidential candidate." Kweon, in custody since September 2025, was indicted in October 2025. The appellate court said Kweon "betrayed the expectations of the public" by accepting the funds.
