Philippine House votes 265-14 to expel Kiko Barzaga, the third representative in history to be expelled
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 2, 2026 (via Philstar, Inquirer, GMA, Manila Bulletin)

The Philippine House of Representatives votes 265-14 with eight abstentions to expel Cavite 4th district representative Kiko Barzaga over disruptive and disorderly behavior following two prior suspensions; Barzaga is the third representative in Philippine history to be expelled.

The vote came at a night plenary of the 20th Congress on Tuesday, 2 June 2026. The chamber adopted committee report No. 298, which recommended expulsion after a complaint found Barzaga repeatedly interrupted sessions, mocked House leaders online, made offensive and defamatory social media posts, conducted unauthorized Facebook live broadcasts from the floor, and repeatedly failed to perform his duties.

Barzaga had been suspended without pay for 60 days in December 2025 and again in February 2026 for gross misconduct and defamation. The court cited Article VI, Section 16(3) of the Constitution and House rules on conduct unbecoming of a member. He was present for the nominal roll call.

The neophyte representative, son of the late Elpidio Barzaga Jr., who first represented Dasmariñas, won the seat in 2025 by fewer than five points. His tenure lasted 338 days, and the expulsion ends the Barzaga family's hold on the seat. It is not yet decided whether he will question the expulsion in court.

The 265 votes in favor exceeded the 257 who voted to impeach vice president Sara Duterte a second time in May.
