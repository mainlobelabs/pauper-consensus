Max Dowman becomes youngest ever Premier League scorer
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 14, 2026 (via Reuters, Premier League)

Arsenal's Max Dowman has become the youngest ever goalscorer in Premier League history, netting in a 2-0 win over Everton on Saturday, March 14, 2026, at the age of 16 years and 73 days.

Dowman opened the scoring in the 74th minute, and Viktor Gyokeres added a second in the 89th to seal the win for Arsenal. The teenager has risen through Arsenal's youth system and was a regular presence in the first-team squad before scoring the record-breaking goal.

The record breaks that of James Vaughan, who scored for Everton against Crystal Palace in 2005 at 16 years and 270 days, a margin of 197 days. Dowman is also Arsenal's youngest ever scorer, surpassing Cesc Fabregas, who scored for the club at 16 years and 212 days.

The win moved Arsenal roughly 10 points clear at the top of the league, after Manchester City drew 1-1 with West Bromwich Albion. The record goal caps a period of rapid development for the teenager, and it was part of a busy weekend of Premier League fixtures in which the result extended Arsenal's lead at the top of the table. Dowman's age record now stands as one of the most durable milestones in the competition's history.
