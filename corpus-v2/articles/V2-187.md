Saudi Aramco and Sonatrach cut July LPG prices
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 1, 2026 (via Reuters, ZAWYA, TradeVAE)

Saudi Aramco has cut its July official selling prices for liquefied petroleum gas, trimming propane 24 percent to $580 per tonne and butane 27 percent to $600 per tonne. Algeria's Sonatrach reduced its prices by 2 to 10 percent, with propane at $518 per tonne and butane at $600 per tonne.

The cuts follow a spring spike driven by a major shutdown at Saudi Arabia's Juaymah LPG terminal and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz in February. Aramco's propane had peaked at $760 per tonne in June, and butane at $820. With post-war flows and alternative shipping routes restoring supply, the July correction is still partial: Aramco's January propane price had been $525 per tonne.

Traders said the cuts reflect increased global supply as post-war flows normalise, and further reductions are possible if supply keeps recovering. Aramco's official prices set the Middle East to Asia-Pacific benchmark, while Sonatrach's guide the Mediterranean and Black Sea markets, including Turkey. Downstream, Pakistan's Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority cut the July price of the 11.8kg LPG cylinder by more than 21 percent.
