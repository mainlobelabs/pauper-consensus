Paraguay defeats Germany on penalties in the World Cup round of 32
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 29, 2026 (via AP, Reuters, BBC)

In association football, Paraguay defeats Germany in penalty shoot-outs during the round of 32, the largest upset since 2018 and the fourth-largest in World Cup history.

Germany and Paraguay drew 1-1 after extra time at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Massachusetts, on Monday, 29 June 2026, and Paraguay won 4-3 on penalties. Enciso's header opener in the 42nd minute was Paraguay's first ever World Cup knockout goal, and Havertz equalised in the 52nd minute.

In the shootout, with Germany going first and never leading, goalkeeper Gill saved Havertz and Woltemade, and Tah blazed over the bar. Paraguay's Sanabria shot wide, and Balbuena was saved by Neuer, but substitute José Canales sealed the win top corner with the first sudden-death kick.

It is Germany's first World Cup shootout defeat in history and its second shootout loss at a major tournament after the Euro 1976 final. Paraguay becomes the first team to beat Germany in a World Cup shootout.

Paraguayan president Santiago Peña declared Tuesday, 30 June, a public holiday, calling it an "epic victory over four-time world champion Germany." Paraguay will face the winner of France and Sweden in the round of 16 in Philadelphia on Saturday, seeking revenge for a 1-0 loss to Germany in the 2002 World Cup round of 16.
