Seahawks sign Jaxon Smith-Njigba to record wide receiver deal
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 23, 2026 (via NFL.com, ESPN)

The Seattle Seahawks have signed wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba to a four-year contract worth 168 million dollars, with at least 120 million guaranteed, making him the highest-paid wide receiver in NFL history.

The deal, announced on Monday, March 23, 2026, surpasses the previous record for a wide receiver held by Ja'Marr Chase. The size of the guaranteed figure is among the largest in the history of the league, and it locks in Seattle's star player for the coming years.

Smith-Njigba won Offensive Player of the Year last season, and he led the Seahawks to a Super Bowl championship during his tenure with the team. A first-round selection in the 2024 draft, he has established himself as one of the sport's premier receivers since entering the league.

The contract is a signal of Seattle's confidence in the receiver's long-term role as the focal point of its offense, and it places him at the top of the pay scale for his position by a wide margin. The extension secures the services of the team's leading playmaker through the 2029 season, and it ends a period of uncertainty over whether the receiver would return to Seattle or seek a bigger contract elsewhere.
