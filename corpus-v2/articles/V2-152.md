Magnitude 7.8 earthquake strikes Mindanao, killing at least 35
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 8, 2026 (via AP, Inquirer, PHIVOLCS)

A magnitude 7.8 earthquake with its epicenter off Maasim, Sarangani, strikes Mindanao, hitting as far as Leyte, North Maluku and North Sulawesi in Indonesia, and Sabah in Malaysia; at least 35 people are killed, over 200 injured, and 12 are missing.

The tectonic quake struck on Monday, 8 June 2026, at 7:37am local time at a depth of 33 kilometres, about 32 kilometres southwest of Maasim. The Philippine volcanological agency PHIVOLCS initially recorded it at magnitude 7.0 before upgrading it. The quake was attributed to subduction along the Cotabato Trench, and tsunami warnings were issued for nine southern provinces, with alerts also sent to Indonesia, Palau, Taiwan, and Papua New Guinea.

Waves of about one metre were observed along southern coasts, with the largest at 1.4 metres at Kiamba. A landslide in Glan, Sarangani, buried houses and killed 13 villagers, and four other villagers also died. Other deaths came from collapsing buildings and debris, including a damaged mosque.

General Santos City felt destructive Intensity 7 shaking, and coastal residents of Samal Island were evacuated. PHIVOLCS recorded 138 aftershocks by 11am, the strongest a magnitude 6.7. Authorities expect the toll to rise as rescue teams reach isolated barangays, with the latest counts putting the injured at nearly 500.
