Russia says Africa Corps freed two men abducted in Niger in 2024
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 21, 2026 (via AP, TASS)

The Russian defence ministry has said that the Africa Corps, a Russia-linked paramilitary force, freed a Russian and a Ukrainian citizen during an operation in Mali, the two having been abducted in Niger in July 2024 by Jama'at Nusrat al-Islam wal-Muslimin. On Tuesday, April 21, 2026, the ministry described the action as a "special operation."

The freed men are Oleg Gret, a Russian born in 1962, and Yuri Yurov, a Ukrainian born in 1970, both employees of a Russian geological exploration company. They were captured in July 2024 at Mbanga in Niger's Tillaberi region, a southwestern area near the Burkina Faso and Mali borders where gold mines operate, and JNIM, an al-Qaeda affiliate, released a video of them in August 2024.

The ministry said the two had been held in Mali for about two years and that their initial examination showed multiple illnesses and severe physical exhaustion. They are to fly to Moscow by Russian military transport for treatment. The Africa Corps, the successor to the Wagner Group, is active in Mali, Equatorial Guinea, and the Central African Republic.

The operation is part of the growing Russian military presence in West and Central Africa. Moscow did not disclose the details of the rescue or the state of the hostages' captors, and it did not confirm whether other hostages remain in the group's custody.
