Messi becomes first player with 30 World Cup appearances and 20 World Cup goals
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 3, 2026 (via Opta, ESPN, FIFA, AP)

In association football, Lionel Messi has become the first player to make 30 FIFA World Cup appearances, score 20 World Cup goals, and score in eight consecutive World Cup matches, after scoring in Argentina's 3-2 extra-time victory over Cape Verde in the round of 32 at Miami Stadium.

Messi scored in the 29th minute, flicking a Lisandro Martinez pass past keeper Vozinha, for his 20th World Cup goal, an all-time record two clear of Kylian Mbappé, and his seventh at this tournament, leading the Golden Boot race. It was his 124th international goal.

His streak of goals in eight consecutive World Cup matches, in which he has 12 goals, runs back through the 2022 title run. He is also the first player to score seven or more goals at two World Cups.

Cape Verde, making its World Cup debut, had drawn all three group matches and is the smallest nation ever in a World Cup knockout match. Duarte equalised in the 59th minute, Martinez made it 2-1 in the 92nd minute, and Sidny Lopes Cabral levelled in the 103rd minute in a goal later voted the tournament's best. In the 111th minute, a Romero header from a Messi corner was touched off Diney Borges for the winning own goal.

Argentina face Egypt in the round of 16.
