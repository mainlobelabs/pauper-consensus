Russian court sentences 19 in Crocus City Hall terror attack case
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 12, 2026 (via BBC, Moscow Times)

A Russian military court has sentenced 19 people for the 2024 Crocus City Hall terrorist attack in Krasnogorsk, Moscow Oblast, which killed 149 people.

On Thursday, March 12, 2026, the Second Western District Military Court in Moscow handed life sentences to 15 defendants, including the four Tajik gunmen who carried out the attack and 11 accomplices. Four other defendants, characterized as facilitators, were sentenced to terms of 19 to 22 years in prison.

The attackers stormed the Crocus City Hall concert venue in Krasnogorsk on March 22, 2024, and opened fire and set fires during a rock concert before escaping. The Islamic State's Caucasus province claimed responsibility for the attack, the deadliest on Russian soil in more than a decade.

The proceedings were held in Moscow rather than in Krasnogorsk, where the venue stands. Survivors of the attack, who include concertgoers and venue staff, were present in the court. The attack remains the deadliest on Russian soil since the Beslan school siege of 2004, and the court's ruling closes one of the largest criminal cases brought in the years since. The sentences carried out at the Moscow court were read to defendants held in a glass enclosure.
