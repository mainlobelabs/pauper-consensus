Sheikh Hasina announces plans to return to Bangladesh from exile in New Delhi
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 5, 2026 (via Sky News, The Guardian, The Economic Times)

Former Bangladeshi prime minister Sheikh Hasina has announced that she plans to return to the country from exile in New Delhi, India, despite facing a death sentence for her role during the violent crackdown on protesters that led to her resignation two years ago.

Hasina, leader of the Awami League, resigned and fled Bangladesh on 5 August 2024 as the July Uprising, a student-led Gen Z movement over the public-sector quota reform, engulfed the country. She landed at Hindon Air Base near Delhi. The uprising killed thousands and ended more than 15 years of Awami League rule.

A court tried Hasina and sentenced her to death over the 2024 student protests and the violence that accompanied them, in a verdict the Guardian reported in August 2025. Hasina has rejected the sentence as politically motivated, and her lawyers have pursued appeals.

Her announcement is the first time she has publicly outlined a plan to come home since fleeing. Analysts said a return would test the government's commitment to the trial and could trigger fresh demonstrations, and it remains unclear whether a homecoming would involve a pardon, a surrender, or both. The government has not yet responded to the announcement.
