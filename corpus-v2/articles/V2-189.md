Vatican excommunicates SSPX clergy over four bishop consecrations
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 2, 2026 (via Vatican News, CNN, NCR, AP)

The Holy See has excommunicated the clergy of the Society of Saint Pius X after the group consecrated four bishops without papal permission, and it has warned the laity who attend SSPX services.

The SSPX consecrated four bishops on Wednesday, 1 July, at its seminary in Écône, Switzerland, in a five-hour Mass attended by around 15,500 people, against the explicit will of Pope Leo XIV. Principal consecrator was Superior General Alfonso de Galareta, with Bernard Fellay as co-consecrator; the new bishops are Pascal Schreiber, Michael Goldade, Michel Poinsette de Sivry, and Marc Hanapier.

In a decree signed on 2 July by prefect Cardinal Víctor Manuel Fernández, all six bishops are excommunicated ipso facto, reserved to the Apostolic See. From now on, all SSPX priests are in schism and subject to excommunication, and lay faithful who formally adhere to the Society are likewise schismatic and excommunicated.

The 2015 concession recognizing SSPX absolutions has been reversed: confession and marriage administered by SSPX clergy are invalid, and the faithful are urged to refrain from participating in the Fraternity's celebrations. The decree echoes the 1988 consecrations by Archbishop Lefebvre, which Pope John Paul II called a schismatic act.
