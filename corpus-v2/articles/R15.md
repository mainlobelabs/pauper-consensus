Kami Rita makes record 32nd Everest summit; Lhakpa Sherpa extends women's record with 11th
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 17, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, BBC, Himalayan Times)

Nepalese Sherpa mountaineer Kami Rita reached the summit of Mount Everest on Sunday, May 17, 2026, for a record-extending 32nd time, while Lhakpa Sherpa extended the women's record with her 11th successful ascent. Both summits were confirmed by the Nepal Department of Tourism.

Kami Rita, 56, who made his first summit in 1994 at age 24 and has climbed almost every year since, reached the top at 10:12 a.m. local time while guiding clients on a 14-peaks expedition, having made his 31st ascent on May 27, 2025. He has also summited Cho Oyu eight times and Manaslu five times, and his closest rival, Pasang Dawa Sherpa, has 30 ascents. Kami Rita has hinted this may be his last climb. Lhakpa Sherpa, 52, the first Nepali woman ever to summit, reached the top at 9:30 a.m., making her the first woman to climb Everest 11 times, and had guided a client from Camp 4 overnight on summit day.

The season has set a record with about 492 permits issued to roughly 1,000 climbers, and a 2025 amendment banned solo 8,000-metre expeditions, with the Everest royalty raised from $11,000 to $15,000.
