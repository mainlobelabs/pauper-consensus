Kimi Antonelli becomes youngest F1 championship leader
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 29, 2026 (via formula1.com, Reuters)

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli has become the youngest driver to lead a Formula 1 World Championship, taking his second consecutive victory at the Japanese Grand Prix on Sunday, March 29, 2026, at 19 years and 216 days old.

The 2006-born Italian started from pole at Suzuka and held off McLaren's Oscar Piastri by 13.722 seconds, with a safety car triggered by a crash involving Bearman shaping the race. The win follows his maiden victory at the Chinese Grand Prix two weeks earlier.

The result gives Antonelli 72 points, nine clear of his teammate George Russell, who has 63. He is the youngest championship leader in the sport's history, passing Lewis Hamilton, who led the championship at 22 in 2007, and he is the first teenager to hold the title lead.

Antonelli was denied his podium champagne celebration, as the drinking age in Japan is 20. The back-to-back victories cap a strong start to the season for the young Italian, who has now won from pole in both of his victories this year and sits at the forefront of the championship battle. The lead puts the pressure on the rest of the grid heading into the European leg of the calendar, where the gaps at the front of the standings are slim.
