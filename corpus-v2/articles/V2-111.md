Frontier Airlines jet strikes and kills a person on takeoff at Denver, injuring 12
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 8, 2026 (via AP, CBS Colorado, CNN)

A Frontier Airlines Airbus A321neo struck and killed a person while taking off from Denver International Airport on Friday, May 8, 2026, at about 11:19 p.m. MDT, igniting an engine fire and forcing an aborted takeoff that injured 12 of the 231 people aboard. Flight 4345, bound for Los Angeles and scheduled to depart at 10:39 p.m., was on runway 17L when it hit the person.

The person, a trespasser whose identity was unconfirmed, had scaled the perimeter fence about two minutes earlier, was at least partially consumed by one engine, filling the cabin with smoke, and was pronounced dead at the scene. A passenger reported the right wing on fire. The pilots rejected the takeoff and stopped on the runway, and the aircraft was evacuated by slide and bus to the terminal. Twelve people were lightly injured, and five were taken to the hospital.

Transportation secretary Sean Duffy said a trespasser deliberately scaled the fence and ran out onto the runway. The Denver Fire Department extinguished the fire, the NTSB and FAA opened investigations, and the runway, closed for the night, reopened around 11 a.m. Saturday.
