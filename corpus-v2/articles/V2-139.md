Senegal's Faye appoints Lo as prime minister, replacing Sonko dismissed May 22
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 25, 2026 (via Reuters, AP, France24)

Senegalese president Bassirou Diomaye Faye appointed economist Ahmadou Al Aminou Lo as prime minister on Monday, May 25, 2026, replacing Ousmane Sonko, whom Faye dismissed on May 22. The decree, read on national television, ended the duties of Sonko and all ministers and dissolved the government.

Lo, who formerly headed the West African central bank's Senegal branch, must form a new government for parliament to approve within three months, and the president cannot dissolve parliament until November. Faye and Sonko were former allies in the PASTEF party, and months of open disagreements, including over IMF loan negotiations and the country's debt crisis, preceded the sacking. Faye criticized Sonko's excessive personalisation of the party, and Sonko accused him of a failure of leadership.

The opposition likened the dismissal to a coup, and Sonko, who was barred from the 2024 presidential run over a defamation conviction and was made prime minister in April 2024 after Faye ran in his stead, had warned in March that his party could go into opposition if Faye strayed from the campaign agenda. On Tuesday, May 26, parliament voted on Sonko's reinstatement as deputy speaker and a new speaker.
