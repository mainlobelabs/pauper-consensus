Death of a Salesman wins six Tonys, the most of any production in 2026
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 7, 2026 (via Hollywood Reporter, Playbill, Variety)

The Broadway play Death of a Salesman wins the most Tony Awards in 2026 with six, including Best Revival of a Play, while Liberation, Ragtime, and Schmigadoon! win four each, including Best Play, Best Revival of a Musical, and Best Musical respectively.

The 79th annual Tony Awards ceremony was held at Radio City Music Hall in New York on Sunday, 7 June 2026, and hosted by P!nk. The revival of Arthur Miller's classic, directed by Joe Mantello and starring Nathan Lane, Laurie Metcalf, and Christopher Abbott, also won Best Direction of a Play, with Metcalf taking Best Actress in a Play. Its six awards set a record for the most won by a play revival.

Ragtime, a revival staged at the New York City Center, won four awards including the leading actor and actress awards for a musical. Schmigadoon! won Best Musical for the book and original score by Cinco Paul. Liberation, by Bess Wohl, took Best Play.

Other winners included Cats: The Jellicle Ball, which brought its first Tony to a trans person in the costume design category. The ceremony was broadcast on CBS and Paramount+.
