Virginie Ruffenach elected president of the Congress of New Caledonia
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 9, 2026 (via gouv.nc, RNZ, The Diplomat)

Virginie Ruffenach of the pro-France coalition has been elected president of the Congress of New Caledonia, defeating Dominique Fochi of the pro-independence bloc in a vote.

Ruffenach, a hardline loyalist of the Rassemblement/Les Loyalistes group, won the first-round contest, which required an absolute majority, with 28 votes to 26. Her opponent was Fochi of the pro-independence bloc "Kanaky NC." The vote took place one week after the 28 June provincial elections, during which the pro-France "Governance" coalition, uniting the loyalist bloc with the kingmaker Eveil Océanien party, was struck.

Ruffenach is the second woman to head the Congress, succeeding Veylma Falaeo of Eveil Océanien, who became the first woman to hold the post in August 2024. The Congress, with 54 members, is the main legislative body of New Caledonia, a self-governing French territory in the Pacific that has held three independence referendums since 2018. It elects its president annually, and the balance of power could shift in coming years.

The election comes after a decade of political turbulence in New Caledonia since 2018, which includes unrest following the 2024 referendums. The next congressional presidential election is due in 2027.
