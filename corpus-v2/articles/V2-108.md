US confirms self-defense strikes on Iran after attacks on three destroyers in the Strait of Hormuz
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 7, 2026 (via CENTCOM, CBS, NBC)

US Central Command confirmed on Thursday, May 7, 2026, that US forces intercepted unprovoked Iranian attacks and responded with self-defense strikes as three US Navy guided-missile destroyers transited the Strait of Hormuz to the Gulf of Oman. Iran used ballistic and cruise missiles, drones, and small boats to target the Arleigh Burke-class destroyers USS Truxtun (DDG-103), USS Rafael Peralta (DDG-115), and USS Mason (DDG-87), with the Revolutionary Guard Navy claiming credit.

CENTCOM said the strikes hit Iranian military facilities, including missile and drone launch sites, command and control locations, and intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance nodes, affecting the port of Bandar Abbas and Qeshm Island. No US assets were struck, although Iran claimed damage to the warships.

The exchange was the most direct US-Iran action since the ceasefire of about April 8. On Monday, May 4, Truxtun and Mason transited the strait through a sustained Iranian barrage, during which CENTCOM destroyed six Iranian vessels. Trump's Project Freedom ship-escort operation, launched May 4-5, had been paused before the strikes. Defense secretary Hegseth said the ceasefire was still in place, and secretary of state Rubio said Operation Epic Fury was over.
