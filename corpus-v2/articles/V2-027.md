Guinea dissolves 40 political parties, including all main opposition groups
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 7, 2026 (via BBC, Al Jazeera)

Guinea's government has dissolved 40 political parties by decree, including the three main opposition groups, on the grounds that they failed to meet legal requirements, and ordered the seizure of their assets.

The decree, issued around March 6 and reported on March 8, 2026, orders the closure of the parties' headquarters and the freezing and seizure of their assets. The three main opposition groups dissolved are the Union of Democratic Forces of Guinea, the Rally of the Guinean People, and the Union of Republican Forces.

The Rally of the Guinean People is led by former president Alpha Conde. The Union of Democratic Forces of Guinea is led by Cellou Dalein Diallo, who is in exile and has called for "direct resistance" to the government.

The dissolutions come under president Mamady Doumbouya, who was sworn in as president in January 2026 after leading a 2021 military takeover. Guinea is one of the world's largest producers of bauxite, and the move sharpens the political crisis in the West African country. Opposition figures have denounced the decree as a bid to eliminate any organized challenge to the ruling party, and several former ministers and local officials have been suspended from their posts in recent months.
