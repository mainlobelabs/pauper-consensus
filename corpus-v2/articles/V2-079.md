Iraq elects Nizar Amidi as president in second round, ending months of deadlock
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 11, 2026 (via AP, INA)

Iraq's Council of Representatives has elected PUK candidate Nizar Amidi as president in the second round of voting, defeating Muthanna Amin of the Kurdistan Islamic Union in a 227-15 vote. On Saturday, April 11, 2026, the chamber cast seven invalid ballots. In the first round, Amidi received 208 votes, short of the two-thirds threshold of 220 of 329.

Incumbent president Abdul Latif Rashid was eligible to run again but did not. The vote ended months of deadlock following the October 2025 parliamentary election, during which the session was postponed twice and the KDP, the State of Law coalition, and Azem boycotted the chamber.

Amidi now has 15 days to name a prime minister nominee, and the Coordination Framework, the dominant Shia bloc, is weighing a nomination of Nouri al-Maliki, the country's former prime minister, for the post.

The selection of the president is the first step in Iraq's process of forming a government, with the prime minister nominee then facing a separate vote in the chamber. The election was closely watched as a test of the country's fragile political balance, and a sign of whether the post-election standoff can be resolved.
