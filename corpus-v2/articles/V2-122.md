Wang Yi says China and US will keep implementing trade deals and set up trade and investment councils
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 15, 2026 (via PRC MFA, White House, Reuters)

Chinese foreign minister Wang Yi said on Friday, May 15, 2026, that China and the United States have agreed to continue implementing previous trade agreements and to establish trade and investment councils, following talks between Chinese leader Xi Jinping and US president Donald Trump in Beijing. Trump's state visit from May 13 to 15 was the first by a US president to China since 2017 and included about nine hours of discussions.

The two sides will address mutual market-access concerns for agricultural products and expand two-way trade under reciprocal tariff reductions. China committed to buying at least $17 billion a year of US farm products and an initial 200 Boeing aircraft, while Treasury secretary Bessent said the board of trade would cut tariffs on roughly $30 billion of non-critical goods.

The White House fact sheet described a chartered US-China board of trade for non-sensitive goods and a board of investment for government-to-government investment, and called for a constructive relationship of strategic stability. Trump said he did not discuss extending the tariff truce at the summit, which expires later in 2026, and invited Xi to a US state dinner in September.
