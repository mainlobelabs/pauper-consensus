Rahul Gandhi detained in New Delhi during education-reform protests
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 21, 2026 (via The Hindu, Al Jazeera, TOI, WION)

Indian police have detained Lok Sabha opposition leader Rahul Gandhi during education-reform protests in New Delhi after he called for prime minister Narendra Modi's resignation; Parliament adjourns as demonstrators demand accountability for examination scandals.

Gandhi, the Congress Lok Sabha leader of the opposition, was among several leaders arrested after a "Chalo PM House" sit-in outside the prime minister's residence at Lok Kalyan Marg, alongside Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, Akhilesh Yadav, and Mallikarjun Kharge. The protest began at 3:30pm, and after about three hours, Delhi police forcibly cleared the area. Gandhi lay on the ground resisting, with a visible nosebleed, and was released that night.

Gandhi demanded that Modi resign, saying the government was "destroying the future of India's youth," and cited "156 paper leaks, zero convictions." The opposition also demanded that education minister Dharmendra Pradhan resign before any debate on the national medical entrance examination.

The protests follow the NEET-UG 2026 question paper leak and the student "Cockroach Janta Party" movement. A 20 July march drew tens of thousands to the streets, police used tear gas and batons, and more than 150 were injured. Both houses of Parliament were repeatedly adjourned the next day amid opposition disruption.
