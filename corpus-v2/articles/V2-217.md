Death toll from Guyana ferry MV Barima sinking rises to 53
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 21, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, Guyana Times, Guyana Inquirer)

The death toll from the sinking of passenger ferry MV Barima off the coast of Guyana two days ago has risen to 53; 77 others were rescued, while over 55 remain missing.

President Mark Phillips, at a briefing on 21 July, said 53 bodies had been recovered. The ferry sank on Saturday night after departing Georgetown for Port Kaituma in Region 1. A distress signal was received at 23:01.

The vessel was about nine nautical miles off the Essequibo coast in waters 13 to 15 metres deep. One hundred and seventy-nine people were aboard, but the manifest listed only 133. The sinking is the latest Caribbean maritime disaster since MV Christena in 1970, which killed 233. US maritime authorities said the ferry was not overloaded, with a capacity of 390 passengers.

The rescue operation involved 16 to 19 vessels and three aircraft, including a French Army unit in Guiana and a Brazilian Navy ship. The captain, Kevin "KP" Price, and two crew members, Rondell Dwayne Roberts and Delon Granderson, tested positive for cannabis and were later charged with murder. Phillips ordered a Commission of Inquiry.
