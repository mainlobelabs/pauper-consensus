Russia claims full control of Luhansk Oblast; Ukraine denies
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 1, 2026 (via AP, Kyiv Independent)

The Russian defense ministry has announced that its forces have taken full control of Luhansk Oblast in eastern Ukraine. On Wednesday, April 1, 2026, the ministry said that "units of the Group of Forces West have completed the liberation of the Luhansk People's Republic."

The Ukrainian military denied the claim. Joint Forces spokesperson Viktor Trehubov told AP that "we only hold small patches there, but those positions have been held by 3rd brigade for a long time." The 3rd Assault Brigade of the 3rd Army Corps continues to hold its last defensive lines at Nadiya, Novoiehorivka, and Hrekivka.

The brigade noted that, symbolically, the Russian ministry announced the complete capture on April 1, April Fools' Day. The Institute for the Study of War assesses that Russia controls 99.84 percent of the oblast, with Ukraine holding the remaining 0.16 percent, and it views the announcement as part of a Kremlin information operation intended to force Ukraine to cede the unoccupied parts of Donetsk Oblast.

It is at least the third such claim by Moscow since 2022. In August 2025, General Valery Gerasimov acknowledged that the oblast was not fully captured, and officials have repeated similar claims in 2022 and 2025 without Ukrainian confirmation.
