Tinubu: all 46 students and staff abducted in Oyo State rescued
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 10, 2026 (via ThisDay, Al Jazeera, Channels TV)

Nigerian president Bola Tinubu has said military, police, and intelligence personnel have rescued all 46 students and staff abducted in May from schools in Oyo State, arresting eight suspects and killing some abductors during the operation.

"I am profoundly happy that our security forces successfully rescued the abducted pupils and teachers from Orire, Ogbomoso in Oyo State today after a military, police and intelligence-driven operation that neutralised some of the terrorists that perpetrated the evil act and the arrest of eight of them," Tinubu said.

Spokesman Bayo Onanuga said, "Finally, all the kidnapped pupils and teachers in Orire, Oyo have been rescued," adding that eight kidnappers were arrested and in DSS custody while some were neutralised, and that "there was no quid pro quo."

Heavily armed gunmen seized dozens of pupils and teachers from three schools in the Esiele and Yawota communities of Oire Local Government Area on 15 May. The victims spent 56 days in captivity. The attack has been attributed to Ansaru, a Boko Haram offshoot. Two teachers were killed, one while escaping and one beheaded in captivity. Tinubu ordered emergency medical care and psychological rehabilitation for the victims.
