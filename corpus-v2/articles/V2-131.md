Aston Villa beat Freiburg 3-0 in Istanbul to win first Europa League title
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 20, 2026 (via UEFA, BBC, Guardian)

In association football, Aston Villa defeated SC Freiburg 3-0 at Beşiktaş Stadium in Istanbul on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, to win their first UEFA Europa League title and second European title after the 1982 European Cup final. At the 8 p.m. local kickoff, Youri Tielemans opened the scoring in the 41st minute with a volley off a Morgan Rogers cross, Emiliano Buendía added a curler at the end of the first half, and Rogers tapped in in the 58th minute.

Captain John McGinn lifted the trophy, which is Villa's first major European trophy in 44 years, and it ends the club's 30-year trophy drought. Prince William watched the final, and nine players from the 1982 European Cup-winning team attended. Onana hit the post, and Ollie Watkins was on the bench as Villa fielded their full attacking line.

Manager Unai Emery won his fifth European competition as a manager across four clubs, and Buendía, whose goal came from a McGinn assist, was named player of the match. Freiburg, making its first European final, drops into Conference League qualifying, and Villa celebrated with an open-top bus parade in Birmingham.
