I Am Maximus wins the Grand National for the second time, matching Red Rum
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 11, 2026 (via Racing Post, BBC)

I Am Maximus, ridden by Irish jockey Paul Townend, wins the Grand National at Aintree on Saturday, April 11, 2026, becoming the second horse since Red Rum to win the race twice. The 10-year-old, trained by Willie Mullins and owned by JP McManus, started at 9-2 favorite carrying a top weight of 11st 12lb, a record for this century.

He beat Iroko, who started at 18-1, and Jordans, who started at 28-1, to take the win. It is his second winning of the Grand National, after his first victory last year, and he becomes the first horse since Red Rum, who won in 1973, 1974, and 1977, to regain the crown.

He is the ninth horse in the history of the race to win it twice, the first since Tiger Roll in 2018 and 2019. For Mullins, it is his third consecutive winning of the race and his fourth overall, making him the first trainer to do so since Vincent O'Brien in 1955, and for McManus it is a record fourth win of the race as owner.

The Grand National is the most famous steeplechase in the world, and the Aintree course is known for its fences, including Becher's Brook and the Chair, over which all the horses must jump in the roughly 4.5-mile race.
