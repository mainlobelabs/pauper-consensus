Iran launches retaliatory strikes on Bahrain and Kuwait, including US Fifth Fleet facilities
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 27, 2026 (via BBC, Reuters, Al Jazeera)

The IRGC Navy and Aerospace Force launch retaliatory strikes against Bahrain and Kuwait, including the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, in response to CENTCOM strikes against Iran near the Strait of Hormuz, which were in response to the attack on the Singapore-flagged container ship Ever Lovely in the strait.

Ballistic missiles and drones struck "eight key pieces of infrastructure" on Saturday, 27 June 2026, including the US Ali Al-Salem Air Base in Kuwait and US Navy Fifth Fleet facilities in Port Salman, Bahrain, according to Iranian state television.

Bahrain's foreign minister called the strikes a "flagrant violation of its sovereignty" and a "threat to security of citizens and residents," reserving the right to defend itself. The Kuwaiti military said air defenses intercepted the incoming drones and missiles. The UAE strongly condemned "the Iranian aggressive attacks on Bahrain using drones."

The chain of events began on Thursday, 25 June, when a drone hit the Ever Lovely near the Oman coast as it exited the strait. Iran said the ship used an "unauthorised route." CENTCOM retaliated on Friday with strikes on Iranian surveillance, communication, air defense, and drone storage facilities, and the US carried out a second round of strikes after a Panamanian-flagged tanker was hit by a projectile.

Tehran called the US strikes "barbaric" and a violation of the UN Charter.
