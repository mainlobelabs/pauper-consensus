Raiders select Fernando Mendoza first overall at the 91st NFL draft in Pittsburgh
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 23, 2026 (via AP, NFL.com)

The 91st NFL draft was held in Pittsburgh, with the Las Vegas Raiders selecting former Indiana Hoosiers quarterback Fernando Mendoza with the first overall pick on Thursday night, April 23, 2026. Mendoza, the 2025 Heisman Trophy winner, "stunningly" led Indiana to its first national championship and rose from an overlooked two-star recruit with one scholarship offer to the top of the draft.

He is the third player in the common draft era to win the Heisman, a national championship, and the number one overall pick, following Joe Burrow and Cam Newton. A quarterback has not been the first overall pick in 33 years, and it is the first first-round quarterback for the Raiders since JaMarcus Russell in 2007, a pick that had been the worst-kept secret for months. General manager John Spytek "played it straight" at the podium, and the selection is the Raiders' second first overall pick ever.

Mendoza skipped the draft in Pittsburgh, where his family lives in the Miami area, and his mother Elsa has multiple sclerosis, and he was introduced in Las Vegas on Friday. He joins first-year head coach Klint Kubiak, the former offensive coordinator of the Super Bowl-winning Seattle Seahawks, and the Raiders also signed veteran quarterback Kirk Cousins in free agency.

The draft is the NFL's annual player selection event, held in April, and the first overall pick is considered the most valuable selection of the three-day event. The Raiders' choice of Mendoza represents a bet on the position of greatest need on their roster, and the selection was widely covered across sports media.
