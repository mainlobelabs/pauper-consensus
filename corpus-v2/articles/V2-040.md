Sinners wins four Oscars, including Best Actor for Michael B. Jordan
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 15, 2026 (via Variety, ABC News)

Sinners has become the most-nominated film in Academy Awards history with a record 16 nominations, and it won four awards at the 98th Academy Awards on Sunday, March 15, 2026.

Michael B. Jordan won Best Actor for his lead performance, the film's most prestigious win of the night. Ryan Coogler won Best Original Screenplay, a first Oscar for the filmmaker. Autumn Durald Arkapaw won Best Cinematography, becoming the first woman to win in the category. Ludwig Göransson took home the award for Best Score.

The 16 nominations for Sinners passed the previous record of 14 held jointly by two earlier films, making it the most-recognized picture in the ceremony's history. The film won in four of the 16 categories in which it was nominated.

The ceremony was held at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood, and the Oscars are voted on by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, whose members cast ballots across more than 20 competitive categories. The film's four wins made it one of the night's most successful entries, and Jordan's acting win was widely regarded as the performance of the awards season, capping a year in which the film became the critical and box-office centerpiece of the year.
