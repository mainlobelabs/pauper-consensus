Vietnam's National Assembly elects To Lam president, Le Minh Hung prime minister
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 7, 2026 (via Reuters, VietnamPlus)

Vietnam's National Assembly has re-elected Communist Party General Secretary To Lam as state president for the 2026-2031 term. At the first session of the 16th National Assembly on Tuesday, April 7, 2026, the 500-member body in Hanoi elected him by 495 of the 495 present, 99 percent of the total, with five members absent.

To Lam, 68, who took the oath at 9:20 a.m., is serving his first term as president. The former head of the public security ministry now holds the top posts of both party and state, making him, by common description, the most powerful leader in Vietnam in decades and reshaping the country's four-pillars leadership model.

The Assembly also elected Le Minh Hung, 55, as prime minister by secret ballot with 495 of 495 present, or 100 percent. The former State Bank governor and Politburo member succeeds Pham Minh Chinh and took the oath in the afternoon. National Assembly chairman Tran Thanh Man was re-elected for a second term.

The nominations were finalized at a late-March party meeting and followed the March 2026 general election. The vote consolidated power in the hands of the party leadership ahead of the new five-year term.
