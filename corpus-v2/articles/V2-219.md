Pope Leo XIV consecrates new bishop of Chifeng under the China framework
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 22, 2026 (via Vatican News, NCR, AsiaNews, Fides)

Pope Leo XIV has consecrated Father Joseph Chang Yanfeng as the bishop of Chifeng in the Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region under the 2018 provisional framework, which cedes authority over its bishops to the Chinese government.

Chang, born on 4 April 1984 in Beizhen, Liaoning, has been a priest for the Chifeng diocese since 2010 and served as diocesan administrator from 2021. The pope appointed him on 15 June 2026, having approved his candidacy under the provisional agreement. The ordination was presided over by Bishop Paul Meng Qinglu of Hohhot, with co-consecrators Anthony Yao Shun of Ulanqab and Joseph Pei Junmin of Liaoning.

The agreement, signed on 22 September 2018 and extended to 2028, cedes authority over the appointment of bishops to the Chinese government. The pope approves candidates presented by Beijing and agrees not to make appointments without prior Chinese approval.

This is the 15th Chinese bishop ordained under the provisional agreement and the first appointment in 2026. The diocese had been vacant for about 20 years. Inner Mongolia has been under China's Law on Promoting Ethnic Unity and Progress, which took effect on 1 July 2026.
