Federal judge blocks RFK Jr.'s vaccine schedule changes
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 16, 2026 (via ABC News, CIDRAP)

A US federal judge has blocked the implementation of a series of vaccine policy changes introduced by Health and Human Services secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., including an overhaul of the childhood immunization schedule.

In an order on Monday, March 16, 2026, US District Judge Brian E. Murphy of Massachusetts issued a preliminary injunction, ruling that the measures are likely to violate the procedures required under federal law. The ruling leaves the previous schedule in effect while the case proceeds.

The blocked changes include a January 2026 revision to the childhood immunization schedule that would reduce the number of recommended vaccines for young children from 17 to 11. The order also halted 13 new appointments to the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices, ACIP, and stopped all ACIP votes taken since the committee was reconstituted.

The legal challenge was brought in July 2025 by six medical organizations, including the American Academy of Pediatrics. The ACIP is the federal body that sets the immunization schedule for children, and its recommendations guide providers across the country. The groups argued that the changes were adopted without the public comment and committee process that federal law requires.
