Dubai International Airport reports 66% drop in March passenger traffic amid Iran war disruption
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 4, 2026 (via Bloomberg, The National, Gulf Times)

Dubai Airports said on Monday, May 4, 2026, that passenger traffic at Dubai International Airport, the world's busiest international hub, fell 65.7 percent in March 2026 to 2.5 million as restrictions on United Arab Emirates airspace disrupted Middle East air traffic after the war with Iran began on February 28. The restrictions, in place from the start of the war, intensified through March, and the airport handled more than 100 missile alarms during the month, about three per day.

Flights were briefly suspended in March after a drone-related incident caused a fire near the terminal. CEO Paul Griffiths said the 100-million-passenger milestone, which had been on track for 99.5 million, now slips to 2027, after the airport set a record 95.2 million in 2025. First-quarter traffic was 18.6 million guests, down 20.6 percent from a year earlier.

The UAE fully lifted its airspace restrictions on April 25, and the recovery phase is ramping. During the disruption from February 28 to April 30, the airport kept 6 million passengers, more than 32,000 movements, and 213,000 tonnes of cargo moving, with its top markets India, Saudi Arabia, and the United Kingdom.
