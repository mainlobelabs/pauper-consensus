Toyota wins the 24 Hours of Le Mans, its first victory in the race since 2022
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 14, 2026 (via BBC Sport, AP, FIA)

In endurance racing, Toyota Racing wins the 24 Hours of Le Mans with drivers Mike Conway, Kamui Kobayashi, and Nyck de Vries, its first victory in the event since 2022 and sixth overall.

The No. 7 Toyota TR010 Hybrid took the 94th running, the third round of the FIA World Endurance Championship, on Sunday, 14 June 2026, after 381 laps, beating the No. 20 BMW M Hybrid V8 by 10.913 seconds, the closest outright finish in WEC history. The No. 8 Toyota was third, 20.4 seconds back.

The win ends Ferrari's three-year winning streak at the race. The No. 12 Cadillac and the No. 51 Ferrari were fourth and fifth, and the No. 35 Alpine sixth.

The No. 7 car suffered a slow puncture and a driveshaft sensor issue early, forcing it into a safe mode that cut about 8km/h of power, and it sat far back on Sunday morning. A late safety car period bunched the field, and Kobayashi, who also serves as team principal, took the lead, with de Vries passing Norman Nato into Mulsanne.

A record crowd of 350,105 watched. The win gives Toyota its 51st WEC victory, restores its manufacturers' and drivers' titles, and is Conway's second Le Mans win and de Vries's first.
