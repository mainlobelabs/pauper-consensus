Messi becomes the first male player to score at six World Cups with a hat-trick
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 16, 2026 (via AP, BBC Sport, Sky Sports)

In association football, Lionel Messi becomes the first male player to appear in six FIFA World Cups and joins Miroslav Klose as the tournament's all-time leading scorer with 16 goals after scoring a hat-trick in Argentina's 3-0 win against Algeria in the opening match.

The 38-year-old scored in the 17th minute from a Rodrigo De Paul pass, tapped in a rebound off a Luca Zidane spill in the 60th minute, and drove a low shot from the edge of the box in the 76th minute at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium in Kansas City on Tuesday, 16 June 2026. It was his first World Cup hat-trick, making him the oldest player to score one and the oldest multi-goal scorer at a World Cup.

Messi, making his 200th cap, was substituted in the 79th minute to a standing ovation from a crowd of 69,045. His goals tied Klose's record of 17 World Cup wins, and the match marked exactly 20 years to his World Cup debut goal against Serbia and Montenegro in 2006.

Defending champions Argentina had lost their previous two World Cup openers, in 1990 and 2022. Australia beat Jordan 3-1 in the other Group J match.
