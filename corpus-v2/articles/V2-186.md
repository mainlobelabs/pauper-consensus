South Korea's National Assembly approves Han Seong-sook as new prime minister
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 30, 2026 (via Yonhap, Korea Herald, Korea JoongAng Daily)

The South Korean National Assembly approves the nomination of Han Seong-sook as South Korea's new prime minister, succeeding Kim Min-seok as the country's first female prime minister in 20 years; the main opposition People Power Party boycotted the vote.

The assembly passed the confirmation motion on Tuesday, 30 June 2026, with 166 votes in favor and one invalid of 167 present, against a requirement of only 151. The Democratic Party, with 161 seats, led the vote, backed by the Rebuilding Korea Party and the Progressive Party. The People Power Party boycotted the plenary, calling her "unfit" over controversies tied to her time as SME minister.

Han, 59, a former CEO of Naver and president Lee Jae Myung's first SMEs and startups minister, becomes the 50th prime minister of South Korea and Lee's second prime minister, 23 days after her nomination.

The opposition's concerns included a personal-info and data leak tied to a government audition-style startup incubation program during her tenure. President Lee approved the appointment late Tuesday night, and her term begins Wednesday, 1 July.

She succeeds Kim Min-seok, who resigned on 7 June to run for Democratic Party leader.
