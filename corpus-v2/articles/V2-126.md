Former Spanish PM Zapatero charged over Plus Ultra bailout money-laundering probe
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 19, 2026 (via El Pais, Politico, AP)

Former Spanish prime minister José Luis Rodríguez Zapatero was charged on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, with money laundering, membership of a criminal organisation, influence peddling, and document forgery, becoming the first former Spanish prime minister charged in a corruption case since the country's return to democracy. The charges come from the probe into the alleged laundering of the 53 million euro public bailout that Pedro Sánchez's government provided to the Venezuela-linked airline Plus Ultra in 2021.

National Court judge José Luis Calama said Zapatero led a stable and hierarchical influence-peddling structure serving third parties, mainly Plus Ultra. About 1.95 million euros in alleged improper commissions flowed to him and his daughters' firm through three companies, with a Dubai shell and a minority contract suspected as the offshore route.

The probe has run since 2024, after requests from France and Switzerland tied to Venezuelan funds, and Plus Ultra's president and CEO were arrested in December 2025. An earlier bailout case closed in 2023, and US HSI is examining the funds' route to the United States. Judicial secrecy was lifted, and Zapatero, who had his office raided, is summoned to testify on June 2.
