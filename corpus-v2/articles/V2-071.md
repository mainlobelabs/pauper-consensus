Wireless Festival cancelled after Kanye West is banned from entering the UK
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 7, 2026 (via BBC, Reuters)

London's Wireless Festival has been cancelled, with all tickets refunded, after the Home Office banned headliner Kanye West from entering the UK. On Tuesday, April 7, 2026, the Home Office refused his entry "on the grounds that his presence would not be conducive to the public good," exercising powers under the Immigration Act.

The decision, taken by Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood, came the day after his ETA was applied for on Monday, April 6, and 15 people have been excluded under these powers in 2024. Festival Republic, which organizes the three-day outdoor festival scheduled for July 10-12, cancelled the entire event; West was set to headline all three days and the lineup was announced on April 1, when "multiple stakeholders were consulted... no concerns were highlighted at the time."

The cancellation followed backlash over West's past antisemitic remarks and celebration of Nazism, including a 2025 song titled "Heil Hitler" and swastika T-shirts worth "tens of millions of dollars." Sponsors Pepsi and Diageo withdrew, and prime minister Keir Starmer said "Kanye West should never have been invited to headline Wireless" and that he "stands firmly with the Jewish community."

A YouGov poll of 4,385 adults found that 57 percent said West should not be allowed into the UK, against 18 percent who said he should, with the remainder giving no clear answer.
