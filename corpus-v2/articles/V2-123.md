Bulgaria wins Eurovision Song Contest with the song Bangaranga by Dara
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 16, 2026 (via Eurovision.com, AP, BBC)

Bulgaria won the 70th Eurovision Song Contest on Saturday, May 16, 2026, with the song "Bangaranga" by 27-year-old singer Dara, taking the grand final at the Wiener Stadthalle in Vienna with 516 points. The act topped both the jury and the televote, the first to do so since 2017, and its 173-point margin over second place is the biggest in the contest's history.

It is Bulgaria's first ever Eurovision win, ending a wait since its first participation in 2005. Austria hosted after winning in 2025 with JJ and "Wasted Love," with the show presented by Victoria Swarovski and Michael Ostrowski. Israel's Noam Bittan, performing "Michelle," finished second with 343 points, ahead of Romania's Alexandra Capitanescu, Australia's Delta Goodrem, and Italy's Sal Da Vinci. The United Kingdom finished last with a null score, and 25 countries competed in the final judged by 35 juries. Israel's inclusion was controversial, and some countries opted out of the vote over it.

The winning song, described by its writers as about a special energy everyone has in themselves, was written by Anne Judith Stokke Wik, Darina Yotova, Dimitris Kontopoulos, and Monoir. The 2027 contest will move to Bulgaria, with broadcaster BNT returning after sitting out 2023 to 2025.
