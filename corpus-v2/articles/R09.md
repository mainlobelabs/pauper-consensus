Turkish police arrest 198 suspects in operations after Istanbul consulate attack
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 8, 2026 (via AFP, Reuters)

Turkish police have arrested 198 suspects across 34 provinces in operations targeting the Islamic State, following a fatal shootout outside the Israeli consulate in Istanbul on April 7. On Wednesday, April 8, 2026, justice minister Akın Gürlek announced the nationwide operation on X, saying it was directed against "the terrorist organisation Daesh." Twelve suspects have been placed in pre-trial detention, including the two wounded gunmen.

On Tuesday, April 7, three gunmen, driving a car rented in Izmit about 86 to 100 km away, opened fire outside the high-rise on Buyukdere Caddesi in the Levent business district on the European side of Besiktas, which houses the Israeli consulate. A gunfight lasting at least 10 minutes left one attacker killed and two wounded and captured, and two police officers were lightly wounded. No Israeli diplomats were present, as Israel withdrew its diplomats after the October 7, 2023 Hamas attacks.

Both Türkiye and Israel declared the incident a "terror" attack. President Erdogan called it a "treacherous attack," and the interior ministry said the gunman who was killed "had connections with a terrorist group," referring to Daesh, the Islamic State. The two wounded gunmen are being held in hospital under pre-trial detention.

The consulate attack is the deadliest incident targeting Israeli interests in Turkey in recent years, and the scale of the arrest operation, covering all 34 provinces of the country, signals that security authorities view the attack as part of a wider network of the group.
