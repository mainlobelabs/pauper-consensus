Israel says it killed a Palestinian Islamic Jihad commander from the Nir Oz attack
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 21, 2026 (via Ynet, Jerusalem Post, Times of Israel)

The Israeli military and Shin Bet confirm that they have killed Palestinian Islamic Jihad commander Zaki Youssef Mahmoud Abu Mustafa, who participated in the kidnapping of 12-year-old Yagil Yaakov during the Nir Oz attack on October 7, 2023, and was also accused of training jihadists at the Nasser Hospital.

Abu Mustafa was the Nukhba commander of PIJ's Khan Yunis brigade. Israel said on Sunday, 21 June 2026, that he was killed on Friday in a precise strike in southern Gaza. The military said he infiltrated Kibbutz Nir Oz on 7 October 2023 and released a photo documenting Yagil Yaakov inside Israeli territory during the kidnapping.

The military said Abu Mustafa played a significant role in rebuilding PIJ's military capabilities in Gaza "in violation of the ceasefire agreement" and carried out advanced terrorist attacks on IDF troops. It also said he attempted terrorist training at Nasser Hospital, exploiting the medical facility, and that he "posed an immediate threat to our forces operating in the area."

In a separate strike, Hamas Nukhba terrorist Mohammed Osama Abd al-Aziz Saba al-Eish was killed. He had been recruiting and training new Hamas terrorists.
