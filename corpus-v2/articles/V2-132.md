Montréal Victoire win first-ever PWHL Walter Cup, beating Ottawa Charge 3-1 in the series
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 20, 2026 (via PWHL.com, CBC, The Athletic)

In ice hockey, the Montréal Victoire defeated the Ottawa Charge 4-0 in Game 4 on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, at the Canadian Tire Centre to win the series 3-1 and claim their first-ever PWHL Walter Cup, becoming the first Canadian team to win the championship. The Minnesota Frost had won the previous two titles. The series scores were 3-2 in overtime, 2-1 in overtime, 1-2, and 4-0, in front of 12,362 at the Canadian Tire Centre.

It was the first all-Canadian final and the first final contested between two teams under female head coaches, Kori Cheverie and Carla MacLeod. Ottawa won only Game 3 of the four-game series. Abby Roque scored twice in Game 4, including a shorthanded goal, Maggie Flaherty became the first player with three Walter Cups, Lina Ljungblom also scored, and Ann-Renée Desbiens recorded a 23-save shutout, her second of the playoffs.

Captain Marie-Philip Poulin, who played with a torn ACL and meniscus, was named playoff MVP with eight points. Ottawa had lost back-to-back finals, and Montréal became the first top seed to win the Cup. Prime minister Mark Carney congratulated the team. The PWHL is in its third season.
