Rex Heuermann admits to killing eight women in New York
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 8, 2026 (via AP, ABC7 New York)

Rex Heuermann has admitted in court to killing eight women and discarding their bodies on Long Island beaches between 1993 and 2010. On Wednesday, April 8, 2026, the 62-year-old Manhattan architect pleaded guilty to the seven charged murders in the Gilgo Beach serial killings case, one of the longest-running murder investigations in New York history.

The victims are named in the plea as Barthelemy, Mack, Taylor, Waterman, A. Costello, Brainard-Barnes, and Costilla, and Heuermann also admitted an eighth killing, that of Karen Vergata, who was not charged. The women were strangled and dismembered, and their remains were scattered on the beaches of Long Island over more than a decade.

The plea carries three consecutive terms of life in prison for first-degree murder and four consecutive terms of 25 years to life for second-degree murder, and it requires cooperation with the FBI. He is expected to receive life in prison without parole at sentencing on June 17, 2026, at Suffolk County Court in Riverhead, before Judge J. Mazzei.

The guilty plea ends a protracted legal process that has continued for years, and the case has drawn sustained attention to the pattern of unsolved killings along the shores of Long Island.
