Papua New Guinea closes Taiwan's representative office, citing One China policy
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 15, 2026 (via ABC Australia, Taipei Times, Reuters, RFA)

Papua New Guinea has announced the immediate closure of Taiwan's representative office in Port Moresby, citing adherence to the One China policy, while foreign minister Justin Tkatchenko said the decision does not affect contracts between Taiwan's CPC Corporation and PNG's liquefied natural gas exporter PNG LNG.

The Marape-Rosso government announced the "immediate cessation of operations and the formal closure of the physical presence of the Chinese Taipei (Taiwan) Economic Office" in the country, a cabinet decision that was communicated to China's ambassador Yang Xiaoguang.

Tkatchenko called the move a "vital and necessary step toward deepening state-to-state trust" with China, reaffirming PNG's foundational One China policy. He told the press that Taipei was informed "weeks ago" and that the Australian foreign minister was consulted.

Taipei lodged a formal protest, calling it a "unilateral decision" without prior consultation, and said the office will "continue to operate normally." Beijing called it a "right decision" that it "highly commends," while the US State Department said it was "deeply concerned." Prime minister James Marape visited China in April, and an economic framework agreement was signed in May. PNG had earlier closed its own trade office in Taipei in 2023 as part of the downgrade.
