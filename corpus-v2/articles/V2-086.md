Iron Maiden, Oasis, Phil Collins, Wu-Tang Clan among 2026 Rock Hall inductees
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 13, 2026 (via AP, NPR)

Billy Idol, Iron Maiden, New Order, Oasis, Phil Collins, Sade, Luther Vandross, and Wu-Tang Clan have been announced as the 2026 inductees of the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in the performers category. The eight acts were revealed on Monday night, April 13, 2026, in a live "American Idol" Rock Hall episode on ABC and Disney+, hosted by Ryan Seacrest and Lionel Richie.

Six of the eight inductees are British acts, a record for a single class. Phil Collins is already in the Hall as a member of Genesis, inducted in 2010, and this is his first nomination as a solo artist. New Order is being honored as a joint act with Joy Division, and Luther Vandross, who has died, and Wu-Tang Clan are first-time nominees.

The inductees were chosen by a vote of more than 1,200 artists, historians, and music professionals, and the induction ceremony is set for November 14, 2026. Other honorees include Queen Latifah, Gram Parsons, Celia Cruz, Fela Kuti, and MC Lyte in the Early Influences category, and Linda Creed, Arif Mardin, Jimmy Miller, and Rick Rubin in the Musical Influence category, the first Latin and African pop recognitions.

The ceremony honors artists who have made a significant impact on the development of rock and roll, and the 2026 class spans five decades of the genre's history, from punk to hip-hop.
