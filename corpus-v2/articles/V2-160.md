Knicks defeat the Spurs 94-90 in Game 5 to win their first title since 1973
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 13, 2026 (via NBA.com, ESPN, NBC Sports)

In basketball, the New York Knicks defeat the San Antonio Spurs 94-90 in Game 5 of the NBA Finals to win their first championship since 1973 and their third overall.

Jalen Brunson scored 45 points, including 13 straight in the fourth quarter, and was named NBA Finals MVP, taking the Bill Russell award. The "Comeback Knicks" rallied from double-digit deficits in all four of their series wins, and in Game 5 they trailed by 16 after falling behind 13-23 in the first quarter at the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio on Saturday, 13 June 2026.

New York took the series 4-1. They won Game 1, 105-95, and Game 2, 105-104, and closed with a 29-point comeback in Game 4, 107-106, while San Antonio won Game 3, 115-111. Trailing by 16 in the decisive game, the Knicks retook the lead in the final stretch and held a four-point margin at the end.

The title ends a 53-year championship drought for a franchise whose previous wins came in 1970 and 1973, ending what ESPN called "your long, long wait" for Knicks fans, who filled the streets of New York after the final horn.
