San Marino elects Alice Mina and Vladimiro Selva as new Captains Regent
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 1, 2026 (via San Marino TV, Libertas)

Alice Mina and Vladimiro Selva have been sworn in as the new Captains Regent of San Marino, beginning their six-month term on Wednesday, April 1, 2026.

The two officials, both serving their first terms, take over from Matteo Rossi and Lorenzo Bugli for the period running from April 1 to October 1, 2026. The ceremony was held at Piazza della Libertà and Palazzo Pubblico, where the oath was read in Latin by State Secretary for the Interior Andrea Belluzzi, and the outgoing captains handed over the Grand Master collars of the Order of San Marino.

Alain Berset, Secretary-General of the Council of Europe, served as the official orator, marking his first visit to the country, known as the Titano. Petar Rajič, dean of the diplomatic corps, made his last appearance in the role before his appointment as Prefect of the Papal Household.

The new Reggenza has identified peace, multilateralism, and the EU integration process with Andorra as its priorities. San Marino's government operates under a unique system in which two citizens serve as joint heads of state for a single semester, a tradition that has continued with few interruptions for more than seven centuries.
