Rosenqvist wins 110th Indianapolis 500 in 0.023-second finish, closest in history
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 24, 2026 (via IMS, IndyCar.com, BBC)

In auto racing, Meyer Shank's Felix Rosenqvist defeated Penske's David Malukas to win the 110th Indianapolis 500 on Sunday, May 24, 2026, giving Meyer Shank its second victory in the event. The margin of victory was 0.0233 seconds on a one-lap, 2.5-mile shootout, the closest finish in Indianapolis 500 history, previous best 0.043 seconds in 1992.

Rosenqvist, 34, of Sweden, drove the No. 60 SiriusXM car and won on a one-lap shootout after a late yellow when two-time race-winner Mick Schumacher hit the wall at Turn 2, unseating Malukas by about a second before the finish line. It was Rosenqvist's 600th career start. It was his first Indianapolis 500 win, and his only other IndyCar victory was at Road America in 2020. The race featured a record 70 lead changes, and the stands were sold out for a second straight year.

Malukas, driving the No. 12 Verizon Team Penske car, had led before the final restart, and Rosenqvist, who had become a father days earlier with his family absent from the track, called it a magical May. Teammate Marcus Armstrong led early in the race, and the field ran 300 laps over about three hours.
