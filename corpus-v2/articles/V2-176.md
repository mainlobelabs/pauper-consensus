Washington Wizards select AJ Dybantsa with the first overall pick in the 80th NBA draft
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 23, 2026 (via NBA.com, AP, CBS Sports)

The 80th NBA draft is held at the Barclays Center in New York City, with the Washington Wizards selecting former Brigham Young University freshman AJ Dybantsa with the first overall pick.

Dybantsa, a 6'9", 19-year-old forward born in Boston, was called by his full name, Anicet Dybantsa Jr., in tribute to his father, who is his manager. He averaged 25.5 points, 6.8 rebounds, and 3.7 assists on 51% shooting at BYU and led the nation in scoring, including a 43-point game that broke the freshman record.

He becomes the first player to lead Division I scoring and go first overall since Glenn Robinson of Purdue in 1994, and the first BYU player selected first overall. He is also the first of a record-tying eighth straight freshman to open a draft.

The Wizards, who won the May draft lottery, have not held the first pick since John Wall in 2010. They had met only two teams before the draft and are adding Dybantsa to a core that includes Trae Young, Anthony Davis, and Alex Sarr after losing about 200 games over the past three seasons.

Utah selected Darryn Peterson second, and Memphis took Cameron Boozer, Carlos Boozer's son, third. Commissioner Adam Silver hailed the champion Knicks and Finals MVP Jalen Brunson in opening remarks.
