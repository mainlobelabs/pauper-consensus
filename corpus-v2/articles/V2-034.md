Polish president announces veto of EU defense loan law
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 12, 2026 (via Reuters)

Polish president Karol Nawrocki has announced that he will veto legislation enabling Poland to access 43.7 billion euros, about 50.3 billion US dollars, in EU loans under the Security Action for Europe program for military spending.

The announcement came on Thursday, March 12, 2026. Poland's allocation is the largest of any participant in the defense borrowing program, which the EU created to fund military and security investment across the bloc.

The government of prime minister Donald Tusk has since pursued an alternative route to the funds through the national development bank BGK, and Poland signed the SAFE loan agreement in May 2026.

Nawrocki won the presidency in a first-round victory in May 2025, and the veto is one of the few remaining levers available to the president under Poland's semi-presidential system. The standoff between the presidency and the Tusk government is the sharpest yet since the change in office last year. The SAFE program is one of the EU's largest defense financing instruments to date, and the veto has raised questions about how other member states will access their own allocations. Poland's allocation funds weapons procurement and ammunition stockpiles under the government's multi-year defense plan.
