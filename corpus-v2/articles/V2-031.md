Bam Adebayo scores 83 points in Heat rout of Wizards
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 10, 2026 (via NBA.com, ESPN)

Miami Heat forward Bam Adebayo scored 83 points in a 150-129 win over the Washington Wizards on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, the second-most points by a player in a single game in NBA history.

The performance, recorded at the Kaseya Center in Miami, is the highest single-game total since Wilt Chamberlain's 100-point game in 1962, and it passes Kobe Bryant's 81-point mark. Adebayo made 36 free throws in the game, a single-game NBA record, and attempted 43, which is also a record.

Adebayo is the third player in NBA history to score at least 80 points in a game, joining Chamberlain and Bryant. The Heat used the scoring outburst to run out comfortable winners against one of the league's weaker teams.

Adebayo, a 6'9" forward and multiple-time NBA All-Star, had spent most of his career as a defensive specialist before developing into a primary scorer. The Heat had been one of the league's top defenses earlier in the season, and Adebayo was a leading candidate for All-NBA first team honors before the game. The Wizards' defensive scheme could not contain his movement off the block, and his scoring surge carried the Heat to a comfortable margin by the final quarter.
