Two US Navy Growler jets collide at Idaho air show; all four crew eject safely
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 17, 2026 (via AP, USNI News)

A collision involving two EA-18G Growler jets of the US Navy's Electronic Attack Squadron 129 occurred at an air show at Mountain Home Air Force Base in Idaho on Sunday, May 17, 2026, and all four crew members escaped and ejected safely. The Growler Demo Team, stood up in 2020 from NAS Whidbey Island, was performing during the Gunfighter Skies air show when the accident happened at about 12:10 p.m. MDT, two miles northwest of the base, 57 miles southwest of Boise.

The trailing jet struck the top of the lead jet's rear fuselage, the two aircraft locked together, rolled vertical, and cartwheeled into a field before exploding into a fireball. The four crew members ejected in Martin-Baker seats about five seconds after impact. One sustained non-life-threatening injuries, the other three were uninjured, and both aircraft were destroyed.

The air show was halted and the remainder of the festival cancelled, and the base was briefly locked down. The Navy is investigating the collision, which was the first mid-air incident at the show since a hang-glider fatality in 2018, and the first edition of the show since 2018.
