IOC lifts suspension of the Russian Olympic Committee ahead of Los Angeles 2028
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 7, 2026 (via NPR, The Hill, ESPN, olympics.com)

The International Olympic Committee has lifted its ban on Russian athletes, allowing them to take part in the upcoming Summer Olympics in Los Angeles.

The IOC executive board on 7 July provisionally lifted the suspension of the Russian Olympic Committee, in force since October 2023, and eased the entry requirements for Russian athletes and teams to IOC events. The decision allows Russian participation at the 2028 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles.

Under the decision, Russian athletes must undergo multiple doping controls and be part of a recognized testing program. The IOC will continue to not organize IOC events in Russia and not invite Russian government or state officials. It is still undecided whether Russian teams may fly the Russian flag or play the Russian anthem.

Before this, Russian athletes competed under the neutral "ROC" designation at Tokyo 2021 and Beijing 2022, and as individual neutral athletes at Paris 2024 and Milan-Cortina 2026. Los Angeles 2028 will be the first US Summer Games since 1996 and the third time the city hosts. The next Games overall are the 2026 Youth Summer Games in Dakar, in October.
