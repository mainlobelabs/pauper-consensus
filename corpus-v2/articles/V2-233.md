RSF commander Mahdi Adam Ismail, known as Jebel Moon, killed in West Darfur
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 4, 2026 (via Sudan Nabaa, Sudan Tribune, Wikipedia)

Rapid Support Forces senior commander and brigadier general Mahdi Adam Ismail, also known as Jebel Moon, has been confirmed killed during clashes with the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Joint Forces in West Darfur.

His death was confirmed by Minni Arko Minnawi, leader of the Sudan Liberation Movement and governor of Darfur state. According to Sudan Nabaa, Ismail commanded Group 128 and the 32nd Artillery Group of the militia's brigade. His death represents a significant loss for the militia structure, given his role in commanding military units and artillery operations.

The killing comes amid a series of RSF leadership losses in Darfur during the ongoing campaign by army-aligned forces. The Joint Forces, a coalition of army units and allied militias operating in Darfur, have been pushing into the region, and the fighting has produced large flows of displaced civilians.

The war, which erupted in April 2023 as a power struggle between the two generals, has killed tens of thousands and displaced millions across Sudan. The RSF, led by Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo, known as Hemedti, has not immediately commented on the killing, and the Sudanese Armed Forces said the operation in West Darfur continues as part of a broader effort to retake the region from the militia.
