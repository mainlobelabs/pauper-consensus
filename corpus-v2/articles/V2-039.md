Kimi Antonelli wins maiden Formula 1 Grand Prix at Chinese GP
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 15, 2026 (via F1.com, The Guardian)

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli has won his first Formula 1 Grand Prix at the Chinese Grand Prix on Sunday, March 15, 2026, taking victory from the front row at the Shanghai International Circuit.

The 19-year-old set his maiden pole position on Saturday, becoming the youngest pole-sitter in the sport's history. He broke the record held by Sebastian Vettel since 2008, which stood at 19 years, 6 months, and 18 days. Antonelli held off his teammate George Russell, who finished second, and Lewis Hamilton, who took third in his first season with Ferrari.

The win makes Antonelli the first driver born in the 21st century to take a Formula 1 victory. The result extends his rapid rise: the Italian had entered the sport as the youngest driver in its history before being promoted to a full race seat at Mercedes.

The Chinese Grand Prix is one of the most closely contested events on the calendar, and the win gives Antonelli a strong platform for the remainder of the season. The race was a key early-season test for the championship contenders, and the result was the season's first victory for a driver who had been a rookie only two years earlier.
