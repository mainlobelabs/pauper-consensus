Philippine senators announce ICC-wanted senator dela Rosa has left Senate building after gunfire
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 14, 2026 (via AP, Rappler, CNN)

Philippine senators announced on Thursday, May 14, 2026, that senator Ronald dela Rosa, 64, who has an arrest warrant from the International Criminal Court for alleged crimes against humanity related to the drug war, left the Senate building at 2:30 a.m., hours after gunshots were heard the previous evening. He left with senator Robin Padilla.

De la Rosa, a former national police chief and top enforcer of ex-president Duterte's drug war, had been under Senate protective custody since May 11 after reappearing and being taken in by allied senators when NBI agents tried to serve the ICC warrant. The warrant, dated November 2025, covers the murder of no fewer than 32 persons between July 2016 and April 2018.

Senate president Alan Peter Cayetano said dela Rosa chose to leave, and no ICC warrant was ever presented to the Senate. Police are investigating whether Wednesday's shooting, in which the sergeant-at-arms fired a warning shot at an NBI agent in the adjacent GSIS building, was staged as a diversion. President Marcos Jr. made a late-night television statement. Ex-president Duterte was arrested in March 2025 and awaits trial at The Hague, and there was no record of dela Rosa leaving the country.
