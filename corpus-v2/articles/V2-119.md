BTS, Madonna, and Shakira to headline first-ever World Cup final halftime show
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 14, 2026 (via FIFA, BBC, Billboard)

South Korean boy band BTS, American singer Madonna, and Colombian singer Shakira were announced as headliners of the first-ever FIFA World Cup final halftime show, set to take place at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey on July 19, 2026, during the tournament's final match between Spain and Argentina. American singer Justin Bieber will also perform, and the show will be curated by Coldplay's Chris Martin.

FIFA said the halftime performance, produced with Global Citizen, Live Nation, and Done+Dusted, supports the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund, which targets $100 million. The stadium in East Rutherford, now rebranded New York New Jersey Stadium, has an 80,000 capacity.

The show will feature Madonna in a jeep with Cristiano Ronaldo and Ronaldinho, BTS's "Dynamite," Bieber's acoustic "Everything Hallelujah," and Shakira and Burna Boy performing the official World Cup song "Dai Dai," with a finale alongside the PS22 Chorus and the Muppets. The halftime interval is expected to run about 27 minutes, the longest in World Cup history, well beyond IFAB's 15-minute maximum. The performance marks BTS's return from a three-year hiatus for military service and gives Bieber his first public performance since Coachella in April.
