Balen Shah's party wins Nepal election with 182 of 275 seats
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 12, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, BBC)

Nepal's election commission has released the official results of the country's general election, in which Balen Shah's Rastriya Swatantra Party won 182 of the 275 seats in the House of Representatives.

The final results, published on Thursday, March 12, 2026, give the RSP a clear parliamentary majority, but two seats short of a two-thirds supermajority. The party's 182 seats comprise 125 won directly by candidate and 57 allocated by proportional representation.

The Nepali Congress finished second with 38 seats, and the CPN-UML took 25. The result ends a period of unstable coalition governments and hands Shah a strong mandate.

Shah, the former mayor of Kathmandu, is set to be sworn in as prime minister on March 27, 2026. The election was the country's largest, with candidates contesting seats across the hills and the plains, and the commission said the counting was completed without significant incidents. Shah had built his party on a platform of economic reform and anti-corruption after his mayoral term. Nepal's constitution requires a two-thirds majority to amend its text, which is why the RSP's shortfall of two seats from the supermajority threshold is politically significant.
