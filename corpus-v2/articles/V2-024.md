US submarine sinks Iranian frigate off Sri Lanka
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 4, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, Reuters)

A United States Navy submarine has torpedoed and sunk the Iranian frigate IRIS Dena in the Indian Ocean off the coast of Sri Lanka, killing at least 87 of the 180 people on board, in one of the most significant naval actions of the war.

The attack was carried out by the USS Charlotte on Tuesday, March 4, 2026. The submarine fired two Mark 46 torpedoes at the frigate, which sank roughly 19 nautical miles off the Sri Lankan port of Galle, within the country's contiguous zone. The Dena had days earlier returned to the region after taking part in India's MILAN 2026 naval exercise.

The Sri Lankan Navy rescued 32 of the crew from the water, while 61 remained missing. Reports of the sinking placed the number of people on board at 180, though some accounts put the figure lower. Later counts of the dead ran higher than the initial toll.

The sinking extended the war to the waters of a neutral country, with Sri Lankan authorities coordinating the rescue and salvage effort from port facilities along the coast. The IRIS Dena was among the newer frigates of the Iranian navy, and its loss is the most significant damage inflicted on an Iranian warship since the war began on February 28.
