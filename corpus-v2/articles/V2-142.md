Melbourne court charges returned Syria mother with membership of Islamic State
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 28, 2026 (via ABC, AP, News.com.au)

The Melbourne Magistrates' Court on Thursday, May 28, 2026, charged 34-year-old Rayann El Houli, who recently returned from Syria, for being a member of a terrorist organisation after joining the Islamic State in the early 2010s. The Melbourne mother of four also faced a charge of entering and remaining in a declared area, each carrying a maximum of 10 years.

Police allege she travelled to Raqqa, Syria, between 2013 and 2014, was detained by Kurdish forces in March 2019, and was held with her family in the al-Hawl camp in north-east Syria before returning to Australia via Lebanon in September 2025. She was arrested at her Melbourne home about eight months later, after being interviewed by police in April. The charges were brought under Operation Kurrajong, the joint AFP-ASIO effort on returned residents.

El Houli appeared in court in a black niqab and was remanded in custody, with a bail application set for Monday, June 1. The prosecution presented an 18-page evidence summary and short videos, and another woman who returned with her was separately charged at Sydney Airport. Since 2019, ten people have been charged over Islamic State links, including three women charged on arrival in May.
