Myanmar junta chief Min Aung Hlaing elected president by Electoral College
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 3, 2026 (via Reuters, Xinhua)

Myanmar's military ruler, Senior General Min Aung Hlaing, has been elected president by the presidential electoral college, receiving 429 votes on Friday, April 3, 2026. The vote, a secret ballot at the first regular session of the 3rd Pyidaungsu Hluttaw in Nay Pyi Taw, was held by all 584 of the 586 eligible members, and 294 votes were required.

His rivals were Nyo Saw, a retired general who serves as junta prime minister, who received 126 votes (21.58 percent) and will become first vice president, and Daw Nan Ni Ni Aye of the Union Solidarity and Development Party in Kayin State, who received 29 votes (4.97 percent) and becomes second vice president, the first woman to hold the office.

Min Aung Hlaing, who has led the junta since the 2021 coup, becomes the 11th president of Myanmar with a five-year term, and the new Union Government is to be inaugurated on April 10. The result is the first presidential election since 2018 and follows the 2025-26 general election, in which the National League for Democracy was barred and the USDP, together with the 25 percent military quota, dominated the legislature.

Congratulatory messages were received from Russian president Vladimir Putin, Chinese president Xi Jinping, and Belarusian president Alexander Lukashenko.
