Child killer Ian Huntley dies in hospital after prison attack
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 8, 2026 (via BBC, The Independent)

Ian Huntley, the convicted child killer who murdered ten-year-olds Holly Wells and Jessica Chapman in 2002, has died in hospital, aged 52.

Huntley died nine days after being attacked by a fellow inmate at HM Prison Frankland in County Durham, England. He suffered severe brain trauma in the assault and was moved to hospital, where he later died.

Huntley was sentenced to life in prison for the Soham murders of the two girls, who were found dead on a railway line after walking to a tuition class. The case became one of the most high-profile criminal failures in British history, because both victims had been in the care of the same family that later claimed to have known Huntley.

The Soham murders in 2002 prompted a major review of the UK's prison and parole systems, and the case led to lasting changes in how child sex offenders are managed. Huntley had been held in prison for the better part of two decades before the attack at Frankland, a category B prison in north-east England. The prison service has confirmed that an internal investigation into the attack has been opened, and the fellow inmate involved is being held separately pending its outcome.
