EFL expels Southampton from play-offs over spying on Middlesbrough training
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 19, 2026 (via BBC Sport, AP, EFL)

The EFL's Independent Disciplinary Commission expelled Southampton from the 2026 EFL play-offs on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, after finding the club guilty of spying on a Middlesbrough training session ahead of the May 12 semi-final win at St Mary's. The club admitted the breaches, which violate the rule against observing another club's training within 72 hours of a fixture.

Southampton's first-team analyst intern filmed Boro's session on Thursday, May 7, from about 200 metres away, and the footage was deleted before the player ran off. Middlesbrough, which had been eliminated after a 0-0 first leg and a 2-1 second-leg loss, was reinstated to face Hull City in the Wembley final on Saturday, May 23. Boro manager Kim Hellberg called the incident cheating.

Southampton was also found guilty of spying on Oxford United and Ipswich Town in earlier fixtures and will receive a four-point deduction, reduced from six, at the start of the 2026-27 season. The club had reached the final under U21 boss Tonda Eckert, who replaced Will Still. The League Arbitration panel dismissed the club's appeal on Wednesday, May 20, and the club's CEO apologized, following the 2019 spygate precedent.
