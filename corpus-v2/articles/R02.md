Helicopter crash in Kenya kills six, including MP Johana Ng'eno
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 28, 2026 (via AP, The Star)

An Airbus Helicopters H125 has crashed in Nandi County, western Kenya, killing all six people on board, including Member of Parliament Johana Ng'eno.

The aircraft, registered 5Y-DSB, crashed and burst into flames at Chepkiep, in Mosop Sub-County, on Saturday, February 28, 2026, at about 16:45, after making an emergency landing in thick fog and drizzle, local police said. Witnesses reported seeing the helicopter descend steeply before impact. The crash site lies in the highland area of the Mau region, where dense weather was reported at the time of the accident.

Police attributed the crash to bad weather. Among the dead were Ng'eno, who represented the Emurua Dikirr constituency, along with a Kenya Forestry Service ranger, a photographer, and a teacher.

The H125 is a light helicopter widely used for utility and tourism flights across Africa. Local authorities led the recovery operation at the crash site, and condolences to Ng'eno's family were offered by government and opposition figures. The highland terrain and weather patterns of the Mau region are known to pose challenges for low-altitude helicopter operations, and Ng'eno had served in parliament since 2017. Her death was followed by a period of official mourning across the constituency.
