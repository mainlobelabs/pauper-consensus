Israel kills Iran's top security official Ali Larijani
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 17, 2026 (via BBC, Al Jazeera)

Israeli forces have killed Ali Larijani, the secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, in an airstrike on Tehran, the most senior Iranian official to be killed since the start of the war.

The strike came in the early hours of Wednesday, March 17, 2026. The attack also killed Larijani's son, Morteza, and the head of his office, Bayat. Iran confirmed the deaths, and Israeli defense minister Katz announced the operation.

In a second strike the same night, Israeli forces killed Gholamreza Soleimani, the commander of the Basij paramilitary force. The two strikes were carried out within hours of each other in the Iranian capital.

Larijani, a former speaker of parliament, had been one of Iran's most experienced negotiators, and he served as the country's lead figure in nuclear talks with the United States. The Supreme National Security Council coordinates Iran's foreign and security policy across its branches of government, and its secretary is among the closest advisers to the supreme leader. Larijani had been a figure in Iranian politics for several decades, holding a range of senior posts, and his death deepens the toll on the country's leadership as the war continues into its fourth week.
