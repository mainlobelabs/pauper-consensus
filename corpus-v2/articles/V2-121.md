US says Israel and Lebanon extend ceasefire 45 days, set military and political talks
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 15, 2026 (via CNBC, Reuters, AP)

The United States announced on Friday, May 15, 2026, that Israel and Lebanon have agreed to extend their ceasefire for 45 days, after two days of highly productive talks in Washington, with security negotiations to begin on May 29 and political negotiations on June 2. State Department spokesman Tommy Pigott said the cessation of hostilities, declared by President Trump on April 16, was extended to enable further progress, as the truce had been set to expire on May 17.

The security track, hosted by Under Secretary for Defense Policy Elbridge Colby, will bring the two countries' first direct military-to-military talks in decades, with the Lebanese delegation led by Brigadier General George Rizkallah, while the political track reconvenes at the State Department. The talks were the third round since Israel escalated operations in Lebanon after Hezbollah missiles on March 2, including a widened ground invasion into the south.

More than 3,200 people have been killed in Lebanon and over 1 million displaced. Hezbollah is not a party to the deal, Israel has carried out new strikes it says are not subject to the truce, and Netanyahu has pushed forces north of the Litani River.
