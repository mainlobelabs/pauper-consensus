CENTCOM: IRGC launches multiple ballistic missiles in surprise attack, all intercepted
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 28, 2026 (via CENTCOM, AP, CNN, Axios)

The US Central Command says that IRGC forces have launched multiple ballistic missiles from Iran in a surprise attack against US forces in the Middle East, and that all missiles were successfully intercepted.

CENTCOM said: "At 5:45 p.m. ET today, Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps forces launched multiple ballistic missiles from Iran in an attempted surprise attack on U.S. forces based in the Middle East. All Iranian missiles were successfully intercepted." US forces "remain vigilant and at a high state of readiness."

The missiles were aimed at a US military base in Jordan, and the Jordanian armed forces said its air defenses intercepted five missiles. This is the first Iranian ballistic-missile attack on a US base since President Trump paused strikes on Friday 24 July to give diplomacy another chance.

Earlier this month, Iran had struck a US air base in Jordan, killing two US service members, which prompted 12 to 13 straight nights of US strikes. On the same day, US and Saudi fighter jets struck multiple terrorist logistics and weapons sites in Iraq, and oil prices jumped. The following evening, CENTCOM carried out a "heavy wave" of strikes on dozens of IRGC targets, including command centers and missile and drone facilities.
