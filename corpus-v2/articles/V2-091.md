Three Japanese soldiers killed in live-fire exercise accident on a Type 10 tank
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 21, 2026 (via Japan Times, AP)

Three Japanese soldiers have been killed and another injured when a shell exploded inside a Type 10 main battle tank during a live fire exercise at the Hijudai Training Area in Kusu, Oita Prefecture, Japan. On Tuesday, April 21, 2026, at about 8:40 a.m. JST, the shell detonated prematurely inside the gun turret before firing during an anti-tank live-fire drill.

The tank involved is the Type 10, Japan's latest domestic main battle tank. The three killed are the commander, Sergeant First Class Kentaro Hamabe, 45, the gunner, Sergeant Shingo Takayama, 31, and the safety officer, Sergeant Kozo Kanai, 30, all of the Western Army Tank Unit at Camp Kusu. The driver, a woman in her 20s, was seriously injured and airlifted to a hospital.

The Ground Self-Defense Force has suspended all Type 10 live-fire drills and Type 90 drills using the same ammunition, and it has set up an investigation committee into the cause, which is not yet known and is described as "rare." The Hijudai range, at about 4,900 to 5,000 hectares, is the largest GSDF range in western Japan.

In a prior incident in August 2025, two GSDF members died at the same range from a lightning strike. The range is shared with US Marine forces based in Okinawa, and the exercise program is a core part of the unit's training cycle.
