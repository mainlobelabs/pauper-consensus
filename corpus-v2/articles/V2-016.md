Mexican forces kill CJNG leader 'El Mencho' Oseguera in Jalisco operation
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 22, 2026 (via AP, The Wall Street Journal)

Nemesio "El Mencho" Oseguera, leader of the Jalisco New Generation Cartel (CJNG), and six gunmen were killed during a Mexican Armed Forces operation near Tapalpa, in the state of Jalisco.

The assault took place on Sunday, February 22, 2026. Oseguera, 59, was fatally wounded in the operation and died during an airlift to Mexico City. Six other cartel members were killed, and three soldiers were injured.

The killing drew a swift and coordinated response from the cartel, which launched retaliatory attacks across multiple states, including road blockades involving burning vehicles and improvised checkpoints. Travelers reported panic at the Puerto Vallarta airport as the blockades disrupted road networks.

The operation was carried out under the government of President Claudia Sheinbaum, with the aid of United States intelligence. Oseguera had long eluded capture despite a U.S. reward of up to 15 million dollars for his arrest.

The CJNG, based in the state of Jalisco for years, is one of Mexico's largest drug cartels and has long been a target of joint enforcement efforts. Jalisco is a key state for both drug cultivation and trafficking, and the cartel has expanded its reach into other states and into the United States.
