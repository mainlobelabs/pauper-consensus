UK fines Reddit £14.5m over children's data
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 24, 2026 (via The Guardian, Reuters)

The United Kingdom's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) has fined American social media network Reddit £14.5 million (about US$19.5 million) for using the data of children under the age of 13 unlawfully and potentially exposing them to inappropriate and harmful content, including pornography.

The fine was announced on Tuesday, February 24, 2026. It is the largest the ICO, the UK's independent authority for information and data protection, has issued for a children's privacy breach.

Reddit introduced age assurance measures in July 2025, but the ICO concluded that the rollout left a window during which children under 13 could create accounts and access content unsuitable for their age.

Reddit said it plans to appeal the decision. The platform's UK user base consists of millions of members who take part in topic-specific communities known as subreddits, many of which are moderated by volunteers. The ICO said the fine was proportionate to the number of children affected and the length of the breach. The fine is issued under the UK's data protection rules, which apply to companies processing the personal data of people in the UK, and it lands amid wider scrutiny of how social platforms verify the ages of young users across the UK and Europe.
