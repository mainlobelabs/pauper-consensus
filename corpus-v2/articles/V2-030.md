NATO conducts military drills as Greenland tensions persist
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 9, 2026 (via Reuters)

NATO is conducting military drills in the region around Greenland involving over 25,000 troops from 14 nations, including Denmark and the United States, in an effort to de-escalate tensions over US president Donald Trump's push to annex the island.

The exercise runs until March 19, 2026. The 25,000-troop figure and the 14-nation participation match the COLD RESPONSE 26 exercise, held from March 9 to 19 mainly in northern Norway and Finland, where roughly 32,500 personnel are taking part including a Finnish element.

Separately, Denmark is leading Operation Arctic Endurance in Greenland, an operation that runs through 2026 and has been stepped up as the standoff over the island's status continues.

Greenland is an autonomous territory of Denmark, and Trump has repeatedly argued that the United States should acquire the island for strategic and economic reasons. The build-up of forces is the largest in the Arctic in recent years, and NATO says the activity is defensive and intended to reassure the region's allies. Arctic Endurance has included patrol deployments, logistics exercises and the use of the island's airfields, which Denmark operates with US forces under a 1951 defense agreement. Greenland's position on the shortest sea route between North America and Europe has made it a focus of great-power attention as Arctic shipping grows.
