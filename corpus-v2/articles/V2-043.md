At least 23 killed in series of bombings in Maiduguri
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 16, 2026 (via Reuters, BBC)

At least 23 people have been killed and 108 injured in a series of suspected suicide bombings at three locations in Maiduguri, the capital of Nigeria's Borno state.

The attacks came on Monday, March 16, 2026, and struck the city's Monday Market, a post office, and the University of Maiduguri Teaching Hospital, local authorities said. The explosions began in the evening, and ambulances and security forces responded to all three sites.

No group has claimed responsibility for the attacks, though the Nigerian military suspects Boko Haram, the Islamist insurgent group that has waged war in the region for more than a decade. Later reports raised the toll, with the national disaster agency putting the number killed at 27 and the injured at 146.

The bombings mark the first major attack on Maiduguri after a year of relative calm. The city has long been the epicenter of the insurgency in northeast Nigeria, and residents described a chaotic scene, with smoke visible over parts of the city into the night. Borno state has borne the brunt of the conflict for more than a decade, and the state government has appealed for calm.
