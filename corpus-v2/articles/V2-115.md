Starmer tells cabinet he has no intention of resigning as Labour revolt grows
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 12, 2026 (via AP, Al Jazeera, France24)

British prime minister Keir Starmer told his cabinet on Tuesday, May 12, 2026, the day before the State Opening of Parliament, that he has no intention of resigning and that the country expects the government to get on with governing, after several ministers and more than 90 Labour MPs called on him to step down or set a timetable for a new party leader. He said there is a process to oust a leader and that it had not been triggered.

Four junior ministers quit in protest, including the housing, communities, and local government minister, who was the first to resign. Starmer took responsibility for the party's heavy defeats in the May 7 English local elections and the May 8 Senedd vote. The revolt began with backbencher Catherine West's Sunday call for a September leadership timetable and has drawn up more than 90 signatures, more than a fifth of the parliamentary party.

A formal challenge requires 81 MPs to back a candidate. A letter signed by more than 100 MPs said there was no time for a leadership contest, while the cabinet is reported to be split over Starmer's future, drawing comparisons to the mass resignations of 2022.
