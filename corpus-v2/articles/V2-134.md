Garden Grove chemical tank overheats on the verge of exploding; over 40,000 evacuated
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 21, 2026 (via AP, Guardian, USA Today)

A chemical tank containing 7,000 US gallons of methyl methacrylate at the GKN Aerospace facility in Garden Grove, California, overheated and was on the verge of exploding on Thursday, May 21, 2026, and more than 40,000 residents in the nearby area were ordered to evacuate. The tank, part of a facility that makes aerospace plastics and engine structures, began venting vapors after a valve failed.

The Orange County Fire Authority said the tank was in the biggest crisis, with either a spill of six to seven thousand gallons or a thermal runaway explosion possible. EPA administrator Lee Zeldin said a tank failure was expected, with a low-volume release the most likely outcome. The evacuation, first issued in the late afternoon and lifted that evening, was expanded Friday morning to parts of Cypress, Stanton, Anaheim, Buena Park, and Westminster, growing to about 50,000 by Sunday.

Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency in Orange County. The tank was decommissioned after it bulged and then cracked overnight Saturday into Sunday, with continuous water-spray cooling, and the evacuation was fully lifted on Tuesday, May 26, with no injuries reported.
