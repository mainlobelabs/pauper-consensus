At least 26 killed as bus plunges into Bangladesh's Padma River
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 25, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, Reuters)

At least 26 people, including seven children, have been killed after a passenger bus carrying about 50 people plunged into the Padma River in central Bangladesh.

The bus went into the water at the Daulatdia ferry terminal in Goalanda, Rajbari district, on Wednesday, March 25, 2026, as passengers were boarding a ferry. Eleven people survived, and rescue teams worked through the following day to pull bodies from the river.

Officials said about 13 people remained missing as the recovery operation continued, with diver teams working around the submerged vehicle. The bus had been travelling from the capital, Dhaka, and local authorities said the cause of the accident was under investigation, with the ferry boarding process being examined.

The Daulatdia crossing is one of the busiest ferry points on the Padma River, and it has been the site of previous accidents, including bus ferries capsizing in earlier years. River transport remains a lifeline for the communities of the central districts, which are crisscrossed by rivers, and safety standards for the vehicles that board the ferries have long been a subject of public debate in Bangladesh, and the accident is expected to renew calls for stricter load limits on the river ferries.
