Louise Arbour is sworn in as the 31st Governor General of Canada
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 8, 2026 (via gg.ca, Globe and Mail, CBC)

Former Supreme Court justice and UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Louise Arbour is sworn in as the 31st Governor General of Canada.

Arbour, 79, was installed as Governor General and Commander-in-Chief of Canada in a ceremony in the Senate chamber in Ottawa on Monday, 8 June 2026. She was then welcomed at Rideau Hall by her family and a guard of honour from the Governor General's Foot Guards.

Arbour replaces Mary Simon, whose five-year term ended. Prime minister Mark Carney announced the appointment in May. King Charles III's approval was the first for a Canadian governor general since his 2022 accession, with the commission under the Great Seal dated 2 June 2026.

Arbour is also Chancellor and Principal Companion of the Order of Canada and Chancellor of the Order of Military Merit. In her installation speech, she said, "The world is watching us, not to copy everything we do, but to draw inspiration."

Carney, who attended with former prime ministers Joe Clark and Jean Chrétien, called her a "guardian of our constitutional order" and a "steward of our tradition of peace, order and good government."
