Golden Tempo wins the 152nd Kentucky Derby, making Cherie DeVaux the first female trainer to win the race
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 2, 2026 (via KentuckyDerby.com, ESPN, AP)

Golden Tempo, a bay colt ridden by Jose Ortiz, won the 152nd Kentucky Derby on Saturday, May 2, 2026, at Churchill Downs in Louisville, completing the 1.25-mile race in 2:02.27. The horse, trained by Cherie DeVaux, rallied from dead last entering the stretch to beat the morning-line favorite Renegade by a neck. DeVaux entered the race as a 23-1 longshot in an 18-horse field.

DeVaux, 44, became the first female trainer to win the Kentucky Derby and only the second woman ever to win a Triple Crown race, after Jena Antonucci won the 2023 Belmont with Arcangelo. The previous best finish for a female trainer was Shelley Riley, whose Casual Lies took second in 1992. Renegade, ridden by Ortiz's brother Irad Ortiz Jr., finished second, and the 70-1 longshot Ocelli was third.

DeVaux, who established her stable eight years ago, had never before saddled a Derby winner, and she is considering a start in the Preakness Stakes on May 16 for the colt, a Kentucky-bred for Phipps Stable and St. Elias Stable. The 152nd running carried a $5 million purse, with a $3.1 million winner's share.
