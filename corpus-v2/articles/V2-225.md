Jacinta Allan resigns as Victorian premier; Ben Carroll elected unanimously
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 28, 2026 (via ABC, The Age, SBS, news.com.au)

In Australia, Victorian premier Jacinta Allan resigns, triggering a leadership spill; Ben Carroll is elected unanimously as the new Premier and Victorian Labor Party leader.

Allan, MP for Bendigo in the role since 10 March 2023, resigned as premier and Labor leader on Tuesday morning, minutes before an expected spill motion she was tipped to lose. The trigger was the CFMEU "Big Build" construction-sector corruption scandal, over which Allan had refused a royal commission.

On Monday, a cross-factional delegation of MPs, including six ministers, told her the party room had lost faith. Deputy premier Ben Carroll, MP for Niddrie since 2012, announced he would contest. Attorney-general Sonya Kilkenny unexpectedly put her hand up but was persuaded to withdraw, and Carroll was elected unanimously.

Carroll was sworn in at Government House at about 12:50pm as Victoria's 50th premier, with transport minister Gabrielle Williams elected deputy premier. His first acts include a royal commission into the construction sector and a special prosecutor. Carroll said: "I am not Daniel Andrews. I am not Jacinta Allan." Allan said she would have backed herself in a membership ballot but stepped aside to spare the party uncertainty. Prime minister Anthony Albanese congratulated Carroll.
