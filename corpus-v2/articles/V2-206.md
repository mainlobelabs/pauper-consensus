Israeli military confirms killing of Hamas commander Anas Hamdan in Khan Yunis
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 17, 2026 (via Times of Israel, Israel National News, JNS)

The Israeli military and Shin Bet have confirmed that they have killed Hamas commander Anas Mahmoud Ahmad Hamdan, who served as a "right-hand man" of former Al-Qassam Brigades commander Mohammed Deif, killed in 2025, in an airstrike in Khan Yunis.

The 16 July strike killed Hamdan, a company commander in the Hamas Khan Yunis Brigade. The joint statement said he "for years, including during the war, served as the right-hand man of senior military wing leaders Muhammad Deif and Rafa'a Salameh." Salameh, former deputy commander, was killed in 2024.

Hamdan was a central figure in holding Israeli hostages and in documenting and filming them during release ceremonies, and he oversaw the release ceremony of Yarden Bibas. Before October 7, 2023, he was tasked with holding hostage Avera (Abeba) Mengistu. He was also responsible for Hamas propaganda in the Khan Yunis Brigade and the grandson of Hamas founder Sheikh Ahmed Nimr Hamdan.

The IDF called the strike a "moral and important closing of the circle against a terrorist who took part in planning and securing the senior Hamas officials responsible for the October 7 massacre."
