Iran's Supreme Leader appoints new military leaders after Israeli and US strikes
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 10, 2026 (via JPost, Al Arabiya, Wikipedia)

Iran's supreme leader Mojtaba Khamenei has appointed new leaders to the country's military, replacing predecessors who were killed in the initial Israeli and US airstrikes, in a series of six decrees issued on Monday.

Major general (pilot) Ali Abdollahi was named chief of staff of the armed forces. Kioumars Heydari was designated as his deputy. Hossein Taeb, a veteran intelligence officer who had been dismissed by former supreme leader Ali Khamenei four years ago as head of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps' intelligence organization, was appointed commander of the Basij.

The decrees also ordered the integration of the Armed Forces General Staff with the Khatam al-Anbiya Central Command. Analysts said the reshuffle filled several of the most important wartime vacancies in the military leadership, but noted that a number of sensitive intelligence posts remain unresolved.

The appointments come amid continuing uncertainty over the supreme leader's health and location since the strikes that opened the war. Taeb, who oversaw the crackdown on protests during his earlier tenure, is seen as a hardliner who would tighten the regime's domestic grip.

The new military leadership faces the task of rebuilding command structures after the leadership decapitation that followed the opening salvos of the conflict.
