South Korean court orders the arrest of Shincheonji leader Lee Man-hee over a party membership drive
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 24, 2026 (via Yonhap, AP, SBS)

A South Korean court orders the arrest of Shincheonji Church of Jesus leader Lee Man-hee for allegedly coercing thousands of followers to join the People Power Party in violation of the Political Parties Act.

Lee, in his mid-90s, arrived at the Seoul Central District Court on Wednesday, 24 June 2026, with a cane and spent three hours in a pre-arrest interrogation. He faces charges of violating Article 42 of the Political Parties Act, which bars coercion to join or leave a party, and obstruction of business.

Investigators suspect Lee forced at least 56,472 church members to join the People Power Party between July 2021 and January 2024 to sway the 2021 presidential primary and the 2024 general election primaries. Testimony said Lee told association leaders before the 2022 election, "Yoon Suk-yeol must become president. I would like for you to have followers join the People Power Party as dues-paying members."

The chain of command ran from a general secretary through branch leaders and pastors to senior, women's, and youth associations under a project called the "Pilates Project." Lee denies the allegations, and the church said he "faithfully cooperated" despite his advanced age, but the court rejected that, citing a risk of evidence destruction.

The arrest comes 169 days after a joint prosecution-police task force launched on 6 January 2026 to probe religion-politics collusion.
