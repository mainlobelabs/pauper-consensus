Spain win the 2026 FIFA World Cup, beating Argentina 1-0 in the final
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 19, 2026 (via FIFA, AP, Sky Sports, BBC, Al Jazeera)

In association football, Spain have won their second FIFA World Cup title after defeating the defending champions Argentina 1-0 after extra time in the final at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey.

Ferran Torres scored the only goal in the 106th minute, finishing a first-time strike into the roof of the net after a Pedro Porro cross and a Nico Williams header. It was his only goal of the tournament. Argentina's Enzo Fernandez was sent off on a second yellow card in first-half stoppage time.

Spain captain Rodri was awarded the Golden Ball as player of the tournament, Unai Simon took the Golden Glove, and Pau Cubarsi won the young player award. Spain, who scored 14 goals on the way to the final, opened the tournament with a 0-0 draw against Cape Verde before wins over Austria, Portugal, Belgium, and France.

The Golden Boot went to France's Kylian Mbappe, who became the first player to win the award at multiple World Cups. Lionel Messi, 39, was denied both a third title and the Golden Boot, which he lost by two goals. Spain's coach Luis de la Fuente, 65, became the oldest coach ever to win the World Cup.
