Florida Legislature passes bill to rename Palm Beach airport for President Trump
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 20, 2026 (via The Guardian, Bloomberg)

The Florida Legislature has passed a measure to formally rename Palm Beach International Airport in Palm Beach County the "President Donald J. Trump International Airport."

The bill passed on February 19, 2026, along party lines. Governor Ron DeSantis signed the measure in March, and the new name took effect on July 9, 2026.

The airport will be the first in the United States to be named for a sitting president. The renaming follows a broader pattern of American public facilities, including post offices and federal buildings, bearing the names of presidents.

The measure assigns the airport the IATA code DJT, replacing its long-standing PBI code, and requires the name to be used on a "perpetual and unrestricted" basis at no cost to the government. Supporters say the name recognizes the president's long-standing ties to South Florida, where he has kept a private residence for years. The new signage will appear at the airport's terminals and on its published materials.

Palm Beach International Airport, on the Atlantic coast of Florida, is one of the busiest general aviation facilities in the country, serving as a hub for business jet traffic and for travel to the Palm Beach resort area.
