Alexandra Eala becomes first Filipino to win a WTA Tour title at the Washington DC Open
Wikipedia Current events portal, August 3, 2026 (via WTA, AP, ESPN, Manila Bulletin)

In tennis, Alexandra Eala has become the first Filipino player, and the first player from Southeast Asia, to win a WTA Tour title after defeating Jessica Pegula 4-6, 6-4, 6-0 in the women's singles final at the 2026 Washington DC Open.

The 21-year-old Eala, born in Quezon City on 23 May 2005, rallied from a set down after a rain delay and dominated the decider at the William H.G. FitzGerald Tennis Center. The Mubadala DC Open, a WTA 500 event played on outdoor hard courts, ran from 27 July to 3 August.

On the way to the final, Eala beat Zheng Qinwen, defending champion Leylah Fernandez, Elina Svitolina, and Naomi Osaka. The Manila Bulletin called the victory a historic Washington triumph, and the title took her to a career-high No. 20 in the rankings, making her the first Filipino to break into the top 20.

Eala, coached by Joan Bosch and a graduate of the Rafa Nadal Academy, is also the first Filipino to defeat top-10 opponents, to reach a major fourth round, doing so at Wimbledon 2026 after beating defending champion Iga Świątek in the third round, and to reach a WTA 1000 semifinal. The 2022 US Open girls' singles champion and 2025 SEA Games gold medalist received a presidential citation from President Marcos on 13 July.

In the men's final the same weekend, Taylor Fritz defeated Rafael Jódar.
