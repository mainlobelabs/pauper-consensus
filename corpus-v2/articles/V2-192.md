FIFA overturns Folarin Balogun's red card suspension after Trump call
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 5, 2026 (via AP, Al Jazeera, Guardian, ESPN)

In association football, FIFA has declared American forward Folarin Balogun eligible to face Belgium following an intervention by US president Donald Trump, suspending his red-card ban under Article 27 of the FIFA Disciplinary Code on a one-year probationary period. It is the first time a World Cup red card suspension has been overturned rather than served.

Balogun, who has three goals at this World Cup, received a straight red in the 64th minute of Wednesday's 2-0 win over Bosnia and Herzegovina for stepping on Tarik Muharemovic's right ankle, an automatic one-match ban. Trump called FIFA president Gianni Infantino after the game, describing the Brazilian referee Rafael Claus as "very suspect."

The independent FIFA disciplinary committee applied Article 27 of the code, suspending the ban for one year on probation. Trump wrote, "Thank you to FIFA for doing what was right, and reversing a great injustice!" Infantino confirmed the call, saying he receives calls from heads of state.

The Belgian FA said it was "astonished," and UEFA called the decision "unprecedented, incomprehensible and unjustifiable," saying FIFA had "crossed a red line." US manager Mauricio Pochettino said, "No, we cannot mix that." Balogun is cleared for Monday's round of 16 match in Seattle.
