Prince Andrew arrested over links to Jeffrey Epstein
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 19, 2026 (via AP, BBC News)

British police have arrested Prince Andrew, Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, on suspicion of misconduct in public office related to his links to convicted American sex offender Jeffrey Epstein.

Thames Valley Police made the arrest at the prince's home in Norfolk on Thursday, February 19, 2026. The Home Office was notified of the arrest 30 minutes in advance. Prince Andrew was held for several hours before being released from police custody without being charged at the time of the arrest.

The arrest came weeks after the release of the Epstein files on January 30, which renewed scrutiny of the prince's relationship with the late financier. Epstein, who died in a New York jail cell in 2019, had maintained a network of wealthy and powerful acquaintances across three decades.

Prince Andrew, the second son of Queen Elizabeth II, has faced questions about his friendship with Epstein for years, and he stepped back from royal duties after the relationship became public.

The misconduct in public office charge relates to the conduct of a public role. A separate investigation is under way into the conduct of former British minister Peter Mandelson, who is also linked to Epstein and is separately under investigation.
