At least 180 killed in airstrike on Iranian elementary school
Wikipedia Current events portal, February 28, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, The New Arab)

An airstrike on an elementary school in Minab, Hormozgan province, Iran, has killed at least 180 people, mostly schoolgirls, on the first day of the war between Iran and the United States and Israel.

The strike destroyed Shajareh Tayyebeh Elementary School, a single-gender school, on Saturday, February 28, 2026. The school stood in the town of Minab, in Iran's Hormozgan province, near the Strait of Hormuz. The province lies on Iran's southeastern coast.

The attack opened a wider war. Israel launched what it called a preemptive strike, killing several senior Iranian officials including supreme leader Ali Khamenei. The United States joined with a "major U.S. combat operation" targeting Iranian missile facilities and naval assets, codenamed Operation Epic Fury, while Israel said its operation was codenamed Operation Roaring Lion.

The death toll was disputed in the days after the strike. Iran's judiciary first reported 85 dead. Later counts reached 156, including 120 schoolchildren, in figures cited by Amnesty International, and Middle East Eye reported 180 dead on March 2. UNICEF said on March 3 that 168 girls were killed at the school.

Amnesty International attributed the strike to the United States military, noting that the school was adjacent to an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps navy compound. Search and rescue operations at the site ended on March 1.
