IRGC reports two tankers hit mines in Strait of Hormuz; CENTCOM disputes
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 18, 2026 (via Times of Israel, Arab News, Marine Insight, Anadolu)

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has reported that two oil tankers have exploded after hitting mines south in the Strait of Hormuz; the US Central Command has disputed the claims as false.

The IRGC said the two tankers, "which were trying to pass through the minefield south of the Strait of Hormuz by deceptive American intelligence agencies, exploded and caught fire." The vessels were not identified, no casualties were disclosed, and there has been no independent confirmation.

CENTCOM posted: "CLAIM: IRGC claims that two oil tankers have exploded in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting mines in the international waterway. FACT: Like most IRGC claims, THIS IS FALSE."

The IRGC also claimed it had "stopped" four violating ships in a combined missile and drone operation, and declared the strait "completely closed" for a week, urging tankers to use only the northern channel close to the Iranian coast.

The US responded by reimposing a navy blockade of Iranian ports and carrying out a seventh consecutive night of airstrikes on ports, bridges, and an airport. The UK Maritime Trade Operations reported a tanker struck by a projectile off Oman's coast on Friday.
