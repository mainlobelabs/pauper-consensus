Former Bangladeshi police chief Benazir Ahmed arrested in Dubai on Interpol Red Notice
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 14, 2026 (via Daily Star, Prothom Alo, Business Standard)

Former Bangladeshi police chief Benazir Ahmed is arrested by police in Dubai after an Interpol Red Notice was issued on crimes against humanity and corruption charges; Bangladeshi home minister Salahuddin Ahmed says extradition proceedings will follow.

Ahmed, who served as inspector general of police from April 2020 to September 2022, was arrested at a shopping mall by Dubai police on Friday, 12 June 2026. Sources said former associates and business rivals tipped off the police. He left Bangladesh with his family on 4 May 2024, three months before Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina's government fell in August 2024.

The Red Notice was first issued in April 2025 for a corruption case over concealed assets, and separate pending charges include extrajudicial killings and enforced disappearances linked to his tenure as head of the Rapid Action Battalion. Ahmed is also sanctioned by the United States.

Home minister Salahuddin Ahmed told parliament on Sunday, 14 June, that Ahmed "will be extradited soon." Under UAE law, a formal extradition request must go through diplomatic channels within 30 days of the arrest.

Officials noted that Ahmed can challenge the extradition in UAE courts, and that his influence may slow the process. Bangladesh is preparing the warrants and documents needed for the transfer.
