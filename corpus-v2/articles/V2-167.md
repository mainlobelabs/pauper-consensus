Philippine Senate elects Sherwin Gatchalian as president, removing Alan Peter Cayetano
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 17, 2026 (via ABS-CBN, Rappler, Philstar)

The Philippine Senate elects Sherwin Gatchalian as Senate president, formally removing Alan Peter Cayetano and ending a leadership dispute that began on June 3; Gatchalian receives the support of all 13 senators present during a special session.

The vote came at a special session of Congress called by President Marcos Jr. on Wednesday, 17 June 2026. Gatchalian, who will be the 34th Senate president, was elected by viva voce after a motion to nominate him. Joel Villanueva defected to give the crucial 13th vote.

Cayetano conceded less than two hours before the session, saying, "I will not stand in the way of that vote," after the news of Villanueva's commitment on Sunday. His roughly 37-day tenure was one of the shortest and most volatile in Senate history, and his nine-member bloc was absent from the chamber.

The dispute began on 3 June, when Gatchalian was installed as acting Senate president under a 1949 precedent after a walkout by the Sotto bloc. Cayetano's bloc boycotted the session and contested it in court, filing a petition on 16 June to void the reorganization.

It is the fourth Senate leadership change in less than a year. The same session confirmed five generals and 85 ambassadors, and Sotto III was elected Senate president pro tempore.
