Nayib Bukele nominated by Nuevas Ideas for a third term
Wikipedia Current events portal, July 12, 2026 (via BBC, Tico Times, WLRN)

Salvadoran president Nayib Bukele has been nominated by his political party Nuevas Ideas to run for a third presidential term after the Legislative Assembly approved amendments in 2025 to abolish presidential term limits.

Bukele, 44, in office since June 2019, received the official nomination of Nuevas Ideas for the February 2027 presidential election, after nationwide internal elections among party affiliates. He had registered on 28 June as the sole pre-candidate, and the results were published on Monday 13 July. Vice president Félix Ulloa was again nominated as his running mate.

The 2025 constitutional reforms, approved and ratified by the Nuevas Ideas supermajority Legislative Assembly, removed presidential term limits entirely, extended the term from five to six years, eliminated runoff elections, and shortened Bukele's current term so that elections fall in February 2027.

Bukele won his second term in 2024 despite a constitutional prohibition, after a top court backed by his allies ruled in 2021 that it was his human right to run again. Eleven parties are eligible, and nine held primaries. Opposition candidates include Mayteé Iraheta of ARENA and Rafael Aguirre of FMLN, while the Vamos party is declining to participate.
