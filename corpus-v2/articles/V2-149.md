Golden Tempo wins the 158th Belmont Stakes, making Cherie DeVaux the second female trainer to win the race
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 6, 2026 (via NYRA, DRF, Times Union, USA Today)

American thoroughbred Golden Tempo wins the 158th Belmont Stakes, five weeks after winning the Kentucky Derby; trainer Cherie DeVaux becomes the second female trainer to train a winner in the third leg of the Triple Crown.

Ridden by Jose Ortiz, Golden Tempo won by 1-1/4 lengths over Commandment, rallying from last place to take the $2 million race at Saratoga Race Course in Saratoga Springs, New York, in 2:03.49. The crowd of 46,128 watched a three-minute rain delay push the post time, and a rainbow appeared over the winner's circle after the finish.

It was the final Belmont Stakes run at the Spa before the race returns to Belmont Park, Long Island, next year. DeVaux, 44, had skipped the Preakness Stakes, saying the time between races was "just enough to refill his tank." She is the first woman to train winners of multiple Triple Crown races, after becoming the first female trainer to win the Kentucky Derby in May 2026.

Born in Saratoga Springs, where she began her training career, DeVaux said the story of the Kentucky Derby "wasn't the horse, it was DeVaux." Her next target is the Travers Stakes on 29 August, possibly via the Haskell Invitational on 18 July.
