Bangladesh reports 344 child measles deaths since March as outbreak deepens
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 11, 2026 (via AFP, Prothom Alo, WHO)

Bangladesh's Directorate General of Health Services reported on Monday, May 11, 2026, that eleven children died of measles in the past 24 hours, bringing the total child deaths to 344 since the outbreak began in March, of which 65 have been laboratory-confirmed. Measles is highly contagious and can be fatal to young, unvaccinated children. The deaths included four children with confirmed measles and seven with measles-like symptoms across Dhaka, Barishal, Sylhet, and Chattogram divisions.

In the same 24-hour window, authorities recorded 282 new confirmed infections and 1,278 children hospitalized with measles-like symptoms, including 496 admissions in Dhaka division alone, and 1,503 children presenting with symptoms. Since March 15, a cumulative 49,159 children have presented with measles-like symptoms, 34,909 have been admitted, 30,862 have been discharged recovered, and 6,819 cases have been laboratory-confirmed.

Seventy-nine percent of cases are under age five, mainly unvaccinated children under two. The World Health Organization notified on April 4 that the outbreak had reached 58 of 64 districts, across all eight divisions, and a mass measles-rubella vaccination campaign has been running since April 5. The death toll reached 512 by May 23, in the deadliest surge in decades.
