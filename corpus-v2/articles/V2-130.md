Somali opposition says President Mohamud can no longer perform key functions; proposes Madobe as acting president
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 19, 2026 (via Somaliland Current, Somalia Today, Hiiraan)

The opposition coalition Somali Future Council said on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, that Somali president Hassan Sheikh Mohamud can no longer perform key presidential functions, including military oversight or dismissing government officials, after his mandate expired on May 15. The council proposes that parliamentary speaker Aden Madobe serve as acting president until elections scheduled for June 30.

The coalition, formed in late 2025 and including former presidents and Puntland and Jubbaland leadership, cited Article 91 of the provisional constitution and declared Mohamud a former president, urging security forces to refuse his executive orders and the government to operate under restrained executive power.

In March 2026, parliament passed constitutional amendments extending the term from four to five years, to May 15, 2027, a move the council called a constitutional coup. US- and internationally facilitated talks in Mogadishu on May 13 to 15 collapsed, with the council blaming Mohamud for rejecting an inclusive transition, and Mohamud insists his mandate runs to 2027 while the government pushes one-person-one-vote elections. Federal forces dissolved South West State in February-March, and al-Shabaab and ISIS remain the security backdrop.
