Clashes in Baidoa kill six as ousted South West State president's forces attack the city
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 30, 2026 (via New Arab, Somali Guardian, Hiiraan)

At least six people were killed during clashes between forces loyal to disputed Somali president Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and ousted South West State president Abdiaziz Laftagareen after they entered the regional capital Baidoa on Saturday, May 30, 2026. Laftagareen's fighters launched a coordinated assault on the city and briefly seized parts of it, including the livestock-market area on the eastern outskirts, where fighting lasted nearly an hour, before federal forces repelled the incursion.

The Somali National Army pushed the loyalists to the rural periphery and seized a military vehicle carrying a twin-barrel anti-aircraft gun, describing the attackers as al-Shabaab-linked. The army was still verifying the casualty count, and Bay police commander Sadiq Dodishe accused Laftagareen of using young men as cannon fodder while his family lives abroad. Calm had been restored, and newly elected state representatives were sworn in despite the fighting.

Laftagareen had been removed on March 30, 2026, by a federal government-backed security operation after he rejected constitutional changes adopted by Mohamud. Earlier skirmishes in May had killed two army officers and seen four military camps briefly seized. The 60th Division held an emergency security meeting on Saturday evening.
