Spurs beat Thunder in Game 7 to reach first NBA Finals since 2014
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 30, 2026 (via Reuters, ESPN, AP)

The San Antonio Spurs defeated the defending champion Oklahoma City Thunder 111-103 in Game 7 on Saturday, May 30, 2026, to win the Western Conference series 4-3 and reach the NBA Finals for the first time since 2014, where they will face the New York Knicks. The win came at Paycom Center in Oklahoma City, where the Thunder had been 4-1 in home Game 7s since relocating. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander had raised the 2025 Finals MVP trophy at the start of the series.

Victor Wembanyama scored 22 points and was named Western Conference Finals MVP after averaging 27.3 points in the series, finishing with at least 20 in every game of the three-round run. Julian Champagnie went 18 of 20 from deep for 20 points, with Stephon Castle adding 16 and De'Aaron Fox 15.

San Antonio became the third road team to win a Game 7 in this postseason, after the Cavaliers and 76ers, and it was the first Western Conference Game 7 since 2018. The Knicks, who swept the Cleveland Cavaliers 4-0, will play Game 1 of the Finals on Wednesday, June 3, in San Antonio.
