Knicks complete a 29-point comeback to beat the Spurs 107-106 in Game 4
Wikipedia Current events portal, June 10, 2026 (via NBA.com, AP, ESPN)

In basketball, the New York Knicks rally from a 29-point deficit to beat the San Antonio Spurs 107-106 in Game 4, taking a 3-1 series lead after OG Anunoby tipped in the game-winning basket with 1.2 seconds remaining; the comeback is the largest in NBA Finals history.

The Spurs led 76-49 at halftime after scoring 57 points on their first 29 possessions, including 11 three-pointers on 16 attempts. The Knicks held San Antonio to 14 points in the third quarter and cut the deficit with a 13-0 run sparked by two free throws from Karl-Anthony Towns.

In the closing seconds, Anunoby blocked De'Aaron Fox's layup, and Jalen Brunson inbounded the ball from about 31 feet over Victor Wembanyama's outstretched arm. Anunoby's tip-in with 1.2 seconds remaining gave New York the win before the Spurs could get a shot up. Victor Wembanyama, the league's defensive player of the year, missed two big free throws in the final minute.

Brunson scored 36 points and Anunoby 33. The Knicks, on the brink of their first title since 1973 and only their third overall, return to San Antonio for Game 5 on Saturday. Fans celebrated in the streets of midtown Manhattan after the final horn.
