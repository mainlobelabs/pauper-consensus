Sudan holds emergency meeting after 308 cholera cases and 77 deaths in West Kordofan
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 31, 2026 (via Sudan Events, allAfrica, Tasis)

Sudan held an emergency meeting led by Health Minister Haitham Mohamed Ibrahim on Sunday, May 31, 2026, after the Federal Ministry of Health reported 308 cholera cases and 77 deaths in West Kordofan since the outbreak began. The coordination meeting in Khartoum, attended by government officials and international partners, reviewed the cholera situation and preparedness for a potential Ebola outbreak, which has emerged in several African countries.

The minister said 99 people had recovered and 32 were under treatment in stable condition. The outbreak is concentrated in the El Nuhud locality, where 215 cases were recorded, and in the gold-mining town of Fuga, where the third consecutive May outbreak has been linked to water scarcity and an influx of displaced people.

The ministry ordered needs assessments and the transport of medical supplies to isolated areas, with vaccination and chlorination under way. Local records suggest 80 to 150 deaths since mid-May, and the UN confirmed more than 300 suspected cases and 77 deaths for May and June. The rainy season looms over the response, and the tally compounds the world's largest humanitarian crisis, in its fourth year of civil war.
