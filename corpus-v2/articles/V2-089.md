US Navy seizes Iranian container ship Touska, first direct action of the Hormuz blockade
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 19, 2026 (via Al Jazeera, Navy Times)

The US Navy destroyer USS Spruance and the 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit have stopped and seized control of the Iranian-flagged container ship Touska, the first direct action during the US naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. On Sunday, April 19, 2026, Spruance (DDG-111) fired 5-inch MK45 rounds into the engine room of the Touska after six hours of warnings.

The 294-meter vessel, operated by IRISL and under US Treasury sanctions since 2019, was steaming at 17 knots toward Bandar Abbas in the northern Arabian Sea, off Chabahar. After the ship was stopped, US Marines of the 31st MEU rappelled from helicopters of the USS Tripoli (LHA-7) and seized the vessel.

The action is the first since the US naval blockade took effect on Monday, April 13, when the United States deployed 10,000 troops, more than 12 warships, and more than 100 aircraft. The Touska is the first non-military Iranian ship hit and the first Iranian cargo vessel captured, and by then CENTCOM had directed 25 commercial vessels to turn around.

Iran called the seizure "piracy" that violated the April 8 ceasefire and vowed retaliation. The blockade is a central instrument of US pressure, and the Strait of Hormuz normally carries about a fifth of the world's oil trade.
