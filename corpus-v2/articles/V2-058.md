Iranian strike damages US early warning aircraft at Saudi base
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 27, 2026 (via NBC News, CNN)

An Iranian attack on Prince Sultan Air Base in Saudi Arabia has injured up to 15 US soldiers and heavily damaged a Boeing E-3 Sentry AWACS aircraft, in one of the war's most direct hits on a US installation.

The strike came on Friday, March 27, 2026, when Iran fired ballistic missiles and drones at the base, roughly 80 miles southeast of Riyadh. Reports described at least six ballistic missiles and 29 drones in the attack.

The E-3 Sentry, one of six based at the installation, was destroyed or heavily damaged, and a military refueling tanker was also damaged. US officials said between 10 and 15 service members were wounded, two of them seriously, and that there were no deaths in the strike.

The attack came after an earlier Iranian strike in early March that killed a US soldier. Prince Sultan Air Base has served as a forward staging post for US air operations in the region for decades, and the blow to its early warning aircraft is the most damaging single hit on US hardware in the war. The base hosts hundreds of US personnel and has been a target of Iranian rhetoric since the war began.
