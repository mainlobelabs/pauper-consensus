Slovenia elects Janez Janša as prime minister after minority coalition wins confidence
Wikipedia Current events portal, May 22, 2026 (via AP, Al Jazeera, Reuters)

Slovenia elected Democratic Party leader Janez Janša as prime minister on Friday, May 22, 2026, after lawmakers approved a minority coalition government, replacing former prime minister Robert Golob of the Freedom Movement. The 90-seat National Assembly voted 51 to 36 for Janša, who would serve his fourth term as prime minister.

The coalition agreement, signed Thursday, May 21, covers SDS, New Slovenia, the Democrats, the Slovenian People's Party, and Focus, 43 of 90 seats, with a confidence-and-supply understanding from the right-wing Resnica party and the two national-minority deputies. The 14-ministry cabinet was approved June 4 by 49-30 and sworn in, with New Slovenia's Jernej Vrtovec and the Democrats' Anže Logar as deputy prime ministers and no portfolio for the People's Party. The government came after the March 22 election, in which Golob's Freedom Movement took 29 seats to SDS's 28, and Golob failed to form a majority.

President Nataša Pirc Musar declined to nominate a candidate on May 19, and 48 deputies invoked a constitutional mechanism to nominate Janša instead. Golob called Janša the greatest threat to Slovenia's sovereignty and democracy, while Janša pledged tax cuts, pro-business reform, and pro-Ukraine EU accession.
