India beats New Zealand by 96 runs to win the T20 World Cup
Wikipedia Current events portal, March 8, 2026 (via Cricinfo, The Athletic)

India has won the 2026 Men's T20 World Cup, beating New Zealand by 96 runs in the final at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad on Sunday, March 8, 2026.

Setting the score, India posted 255 for the loss of five wickets, with Samson scoring 89. New Zealand, chasing the target, were bowled out for 159. The total of 255 for five is the highest ever posted in a T20 World Cup final. Jasprit Bumrah was named player of the tournament.

The title is India's third in the tournament's history, following victories in 2007 and 2024, and it makes India the first team to win back-to-back T20 World Cups. It is also the first time a host nation has won the event.

The final drew a crowd of 86,824 at the Narendra Modi Stadium, one of the largest venues in international cricket. India's dominance of the tournament was reflected in its run of victories through to the decider. New Zealand had reached the final on the back of a clean sweep of its group and knockout matches, and its campaign was praised even by the opponents it lost to.
