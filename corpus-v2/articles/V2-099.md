Nelly Korda wins the Chevron Championship by five strokes, regains world No. 1
Wikipedia Current events portal, April 26, 2026 (via AP, LPGA)

Nelly Korda has won the Chevron Championship at Memorial Park in Houston by five strokes, securing her third major title and regaining the world number one ranking. On Sunday, April 26, 2026, Korda, 27, of the United States, shot rounds of 65, 65, 70, and 70, finishing 18 under par at 270 to win by five over Patty Tavatanakit and Ruoning Yin, who tied for second.

The tournament, a new home for the event, was played at the par-72, 6,811-yard Memorial Park with a USD 9 million purse. Korda led wire to wire, her lead never under four strokes and building to eight, she shot even par over the last 29 holes, and she led the field with 23 birdies while holding 59 of 72 greens in regulation. The five-stroke margin is the largest at this major in 18 years.

It is Korda's third major title and her second winning of the Chevron, after her first in 2024 at Carlton Woods, making her the first American woman with three major titles since Meg Mallon in 2000. It is her 17th win on the LPGA Tour and her 21st win worldwide, and she earned USD 1.35 million.

The victory returned Korda to the number one position in the world rankings, which she first held in August 2025, taking it back from Jeeno Thitikul. At the final hole, she cannonballed into the temporary 18th-hole plunge pool, a celebration she also performed after her 2024 win.
