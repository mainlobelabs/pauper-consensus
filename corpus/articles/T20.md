JDT set world record with 109-game unbeaten league run

KUALA LUMPUR (Reuters) — Malaysia's Johor Darul Ta'zim (JDT) set a new world record on Friday, extending their unbeaten run in top-flight league football to 109 matches after a 3-0 victory over Kuching City in the opening match of the 2026/27 Malaysian Super League season.

Marcos Guilherme scored in the 11th minute, and Oscar Arribas and Shahab Zahedi found the net in the second half as the 12-time defending champions won in front of a packed crowd at Sultan Ibrahim Stadium.

The win took JDT one game clear of the previous mark of 108 top-flight league games without defeat, set by Ivory Coast's ASEC Mimosas between 1989 and 1994. JDT had matched that record in May with a 14-1 demolition of Kelantan The Real Warriors, the biggest winning margin in the league's history, in the final match of last season.

The Southern Tigers have not lost a league match since a 1-0 defeat by Terengganu in April 2021, and have gone unbeaten through the last four Super League campaigns. In the run's only serious scare, they came from 2-1 down against Sri Pahang in May 2022 before all-time record scorer Bergson levelled in the 88th minute.

The club is owned by Johor Crown Prince Tunku Ismail, whose arrival more than a decade ago set the team on its path of dominance, and is managed by the Spanish coach Xisco Munoz.

JDT were also drawn 3-3 against Chelsea in a preseason friendly this summer, and remain the only Southeast Asian club to have won the AFC Cup, in 2015.
