The Odyssey becomes highest-grossing R-rated film of all time

LOS ANGELES (Variety) — Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" is officially the highest-grossing R-rated movie of all time, with $1.352 billion worldwide, overtaking the benchmark set by 2024's "Deadpool & Wolverine" at $1.338 billion.

The blockbuster landed in theaters on July 17, collecting roughly $123 million in its domestic debut, and has since generated $516 million domestically and $836 million internationally. The nearly three-hour adaptation of Homer's epic poem, rated R for violence and some language, stars Matt Damon as Odysseus and Anne Hathaway as Penelope.

The total makes "The Odyssey" Nolan's biggest movie of all time, surpassing 2012's "The Dark Knight Rises" at $1.08 billion, and it is also IMAX's highest-grossing release of all time, outpacing 2009's "Avatar" at $271 million in that format.

The film crossed $1 billion on August 7, making it only the third R-rated movie ever to reach that benchmark, after "Deadpool & Wolverine" and "Joker." Ryan Reynolds posted a congratulatory video after the milestone.

"Deadpool & Wolverine" still holds the record for the highest opening weekend for an R-rated film at $444.7 million worldwide. For the year to date, "The Odyssey" is the second-highest-grossing film in the world behind "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" at $2.039 billion.
