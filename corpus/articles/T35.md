Ukraine launches one of its largest aerial attacks of the war, killing at least 6 in Russia

KYIV/MOSCOW (AP) — Ukraine launched hundreds of drones across Russia early on Sunday, killing at least six people in one of Kyiv's largest aerial attacks of the war.

Russia's Ministry of Defense said it had destroyed 822 Ukrainian drones overnight, the largest attack of 2026 by Russian officials' account. Some 600 drones were detected headed toward the Russian capital, Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said, with a third of those destroyed over the Moscow region itself.

A Wildberries logistics facility and warehouse near Moscow, in the town of Koledino in the Moscow region, was hit and burning.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the strikes were aimed at disrupting Russian military logistics and war infrastructure, and that Russia had launched more than 1,500 drones into Ukraine over the past week.

Russian attacks also struck Ukraine on Sunday. A missile hit a book market in Kyiv, setting kiosks near the Pochaina metro station on fire and wounding six people, and fires broke out across the capital. Zelenskyy wrote that a Russian missile attack on Kryvyi Rih killed two people and wounded 14 others, and that one more person had been killed in the city of Sumy.

The war is now in its roughly fourth-and-a-half year, and Ukraine has increasingly been striking refineries, industrial plants and logistics depots deep inside Russia with long-range missiles and drone swarms.
